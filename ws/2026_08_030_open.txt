== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening process was designed to filter out highly volatile, momentum-driven stocks and mega-cap market leaders, focusing instead on high-quality small-to-mid-cap companies ($500M to $50B market cap) with durable competitive advantages. We prioritized companies with consistent revenue and earnings growth, strong balance sheets (low debt-to-equity), and attractive valuations relative to their growth potential (PEG < 1.5). Key structural themes informing our selection include the modernization of the US electrical grid, municipal water infrastructure upgrades, demographic tailwinds in senior healthcare, industrial automation, and the global nuclear energy renaissance. By focusing on niche market leaders with high customer switching costs and essential product offerings, this watchlist identifies overlooked compounders capable of delivering superior long-term risk-adjusted returns.

== 選股方法論 ==
篩選流程旨在過濾掉高波動性、動能驅動的股票以及超大型市值市場龍頭，轉而專注於具有持久競爭優勢的高品質中小型股（市值介於 5 億至 500 億美元之間）。我們優先選擇營收與盈餘持續成長、資產負債表強健（低負債權益比），且相較於其成長潛力估值具吸引力（PEG < 1.5）的公司。引導我們選股的關鍵結構性主題包括：美國電網現代化、城市水務基礎設施升級、老年醫療保健的人口結構紅利、工業自動化以及全球核能復興。透過專注於具有高客戶轉換成本和必需產品的利基市場領導者，此觀察名單識別出被市場忽視的優質複利企業，這些企業有能力提供卓越的長期風險調整後報酬。

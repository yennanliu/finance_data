== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening process deliberately excludes mega-cap technology leaders, high-volume momentum darlings, and speculative retail-hyped equities to focus exclusively on quality compounders across the small-to-mid-cap spectrum ($500M to $50B). Financial screening filters required healthy balance sheets, positive free cash flow generation, and attractive valuation metrics anchored by a PEG ratio benchmark around 1.0–1.5. Quantitative metrics were balanced with qualitative economic moat assessments, emphasizing high regulatory barriers, specialized technical expertise, embedded B2B customer relationships, and structural tailwinds such as U.S. manufacturing re-shoring, AI infrastructure buildouts, and demographically driven healthcare demand.

== 選股方法論 ==
本選股流程刻意排除巨型科技龍頭、高交易量的熱門動量股以及散戶投機炒作的標的，專注於中小市值區間（5億至500億美元）的高品質複合成長企業。財務篩選標準要求具備健康的資產負債表、正向的自由現金流生成能力，以及以 1.0–1.5 左右的本益成長比（PEG）為基準的具吸引力估值指標。量化指標與質化經濟護城河評估相結合，重點關注高監管壁壘、專業技術知識、深厚嵌入的 B2B 客戶關係，以及美國製造業回流、AI 基礎設施建設與人口結構驅動的醫療需求等結構性尾風。

== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
This watchlist filters for high-quality US mid- and small-cap compounders ($500M–$50B market cap) while deliberately excluding S&P 500 top-50 mega-caps, high-beta momentum stocks, and media-hyped retail favorites. Screening criteria prioritized companies with durable competitive moats (e.g., sole-source defense technology, mission-critical industrial components, proprietary distributions, high switching-cost software), clean balance sheets with low leverage (Debt/Equity < 0.70), consistent free cash flow generation, and growth-at-a-reasonable-price (PEG < 2.0). Macro and secular themes informing these selections include grid modernization, manufacturing reshoring, energy transition, municipal infrastructure replacement, healthcare technology conversion, and automated industrial workflows.

== 選股方法論 ==
本觀察名單旨在篩選優質的美國中小型複利成長股（市值介於 5 億至 500 億美元之間），並刻意排除標普 500 前 50 大巨型科技股、高貝塔動能股以及媒體過度炒作的散戶熱門股。選股條件優先考量具備持久競爭護城河的公司（如獨家供應國防科技、關鍵工業零組件、專利分銷網絡、高轉換成本之軟體系統）、資產負債表健全且低負債比率（負債權益比 < 0.70）、穩定產生自由現金流，以及合理價格下的成長性（PEG < 2.0）。主導本次選股的宏觀與產業結構性趨勢包括：電網現代化、製造業回流、能源轉型、地方水務基礎建設更新、醫療科技轉型以及工業自動化工作流程。

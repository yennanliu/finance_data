== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
Our screening process is designed to filter out market noise and focus purely on high-quality, under-the-radar compounders. We excluded any company with a market cap exceeding $50 billion or those frequently featured in mainstream financial media to avoid "hype premium" valuations. We prioritized businesses with a PEG ratio below 1.5, positive free cash flow, and low-to-moderate debt-to-equity ratios. Our sector themes focused heavily on structural tailwinds such as domestic manufacturing reshoring, smart water and grid infrastructure, advanced semiconductor packaging, and demographic shifts in healthcare. By balancing robust organic growth with reasonable valuations, this watchlist identifies resilient businesses capable of compounding capital over a multi-year horizon regardless of macro volatility.

== 選股方法論 ==
我們的篩選流程旨在過濾市場噪音，純粹專注於高品質、未受市場矚目的複利型企業。我們排除了市值超過 500 億美元的公司，以及經常出現在主流財經媒體熱門名單上的股票，以避免「炒作溢價」的估值。我們優先考慮本益成長比（PEG）低於 1.5、自由現金流為正且負債權益比較低的企業。我們的產業主題重度聚焦於結構性順風，例如本土製造業回流、智慧水務與電網基礎設施、先進半導體封裝以及醫療保健領域的人口結構變化。通過在強勁的有機成長與合理的估值之間取得平衡，本觀察名單識別出了無論宏觀環境如何波動，均能實現多年資本複利的具備韌性的企業。

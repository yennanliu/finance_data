== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening process focused on identifying high-quality, under-the-radar mid-and-small-cap companies ($500M to $50B) while systematically filtering out mega-caps and highly volatile momentum stocks. Key filters excluded any stock with excessive retail trading volume, extreme valuation multiples (P/E > 40x without exceptional growth), and high short interest. Instead, the methodology prioritized companies with strong balance sheets (low debt-to-equity), consistent free cash flow generation, and high returns on invested capital. Growth was weighted relative to valuation, targeting a PEG ratio below 1.5 where possible to ensure a strong margin of safety. The selection is heavily informed by structural, long-term macro themes, including infrastructure modernization, water scarcity solutions, advanced manufacturing automation, defense spending, and demographic shifts.

== 選股方法論 ==
篩選過程專注於識別優質、被忽視的中小型公司（市值在 5 億美元至 500 億美元之間），同時系統性地過濾掉巨型股和高波動性的動能股。關鍵過濾條件排除了任何散戶交易量過大、估值倍數極端（在沒有異常成長的情況下本益比 > 40 倍）以及放空比率高的股票。相反，該方法論優先考慮資產負債表強健（低負債權益比）、持續產生自由現金流且投資資本回報率高的公司。成長性相對於估值進行了權衡，盡可能將本益成長比 (PEG) 控制在 1.5 以下，以確保強大的安全邊際。選股在很大程度上受到結構性、長期宏觀主題的啟發，包括基礎設施現代化、水資源短缺解決方案、先進製造自動化、國防開支和人口結構轉變。

== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening logic used to build this watchlist focused on identifying high-quality, under-the-radar compounders while filtering out overvalued, momentum-driven market darlings. First, we excluded any company with a market cap exceeding $50 billion or those ranked in the S&P 500 top-50, focusing on the small-to-mid-cap sweet spot ($500M–$50B) where structural mispricings are most common. Second, we filtered out companies with high retail sentiment, meme-stock characteristics, or excessive media coverage to ensure a contrarian, value-oriented selection. Third, we prioritized companies with strong balance sheets (low debt-to-equity ratios and positive free cash flow) and robust fundamental growth drivers, targeting a forward PEG ratio below 1.5. Finally, we weighted our selection toward companies with clear, identifiable competitive moats (such as high switching costs, regulatory barriers, or proprietary technology) that are positioned to benefit from long-term structural tailwinds, including domestic manufacturing reshoring, demographic aging, water scarcity, and specialized technology niches.

== 選股方法論 ==
建立此觀察名單的篩選邏輯側重於識別高質量、被市場忽視的複利成長股，同時過濾掉估值過高、由動能驅動的市場寵兒。首先，我們排除了任何市值超過 500 億美元或標普 500 指數前 50 名的公司，將重點放在結構性定價偏差最常見的中小型股黃金區間（5 億至 500 億美元）。其次，我們過濾掉了散戶情緒高漲、具備迷因股特徵或媒體過度報導的公司，以確保選擇具備逆向投資和價值導向的特質。第三，我們優先考慮資產負債表強健（低負債權益比和正自由現金流）且具備強勁基本面成長驅動力的公司，目標預估本益成長比（PEG）低於 1.5。最後，我們將權重傾向於具有明確、可識別競爭護城河（如高轉換成本、監管壁壘或專有技術）的公司，這些公司有望從長期結構性順風中受益，包括國內製造業回流、人口老齡化、水資源短缺以及專業科技利基市場。

== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The selection methodology focused on identifying high-quality, mid-sized compounders ($500M to $50B market cap) that operate in niche markets with high barriers to entry, avoiding the valuation premium and media noise associated with mega-cap tech and momentum stocks. We screened for companies with strong balance sheets (low debt-to-equity), consistent free cash flow generation, and a forward PEG ratio ideally below 1.5, ensuring growth is purchased at a reasonable price. Weight was heavily given to structural tailwinds—such as infrastructure reshoring, water resource management, advanced semiconductor packaging, and specialized regulatory moats—which provide secular growth independent of short-term macroeconomic volatility.

== 選股方法論 ==
選股方法論著重於尋找在具有高進入門檻的利基市場中運營的高品質、中型複利型企業（市值介於 5 億至 500 億美元之間），以避開與超大型科技股和動能股相關的估值溢價和媒體雜音。我們篩選出資產負債表強健（低負債權益比）、能持續產生自由現金流，且預估本益成長比（PEG）理想上低於 1.5 的公司，以確保以合理的價格獲取成長。我們高度權衡了結構性順風因素——例如基礎設施回流、水資源管理、先進半導體封裝以及專業的法規護城河——這些因素提供了獨立於短期總體經濟波動之外的長期成長動能。

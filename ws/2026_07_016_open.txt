The selection methodology focused on identifying high-quality, mid-and-small-cap compounders that operate in niche markets with high barriers to entry. Mainstream "hot" stocks and momentum plays were filtered out by excluding companies with excessive valuations (PEG > 1.5 where possible, or extreme forward P/E ratios unsupported by cash flows) and those frequently trending in retail trading forums. A balanced approach was taken to weight structural growth (such as infrastructure modernization, demographic shifts, and automation) against valuation discipline, prioritizing companies with net-cash or low-debt balance sheets and high free cash flow yields. Sector themes heavily favored mission-critical industrial services, specialized healthcare, and B2B software/technology providers with high customer switching costs.

== 選股方法論 ==
本選股方法論專注於尋找在具有高准入門檻的利基市場中營運、且具備高優質中小型市值特質的複利企業。我們排除了估值過高（盡可能要求本益成長比 PEG < 1.5，或缺乏現金流支持的極端預估本益比）以及在散戶論壇中頻繁討論的熱門股與動能股。本方法採用平衡策略，在結構性成長（如基礎設施現代化、人口結構轉變和自動化）與估值紀律之間進行權衡，優先考慮擁有淨現金或低負債資產負債表以及高自由現金流殖利率的公司。產業主題則高度青睞關鍵任務型工業服務、專業醫療保健，以及具備高客戶轉換成本的 B2B 軟體與技術供應商。

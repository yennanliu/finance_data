== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
Our screening methodology focused on identifying high-quality, mid-and-small-cap companies ($500M to $50B) that operate outside the spotlight of mainstream financial media and momentum-driven retail trading. We systematically filtered out companies with extreme valuations (P/E > 30x or PEG > 1.5), high debt-to-equity ratios (>1.0), or negative free cash flows. Instead, we prioritized businesses with durable competitive moats—such as high switching costs, proprietary technologies, or regulatory protections—and strong balance sheets. We balanced growth and value by selecting companies with consistent, double-digit forward EPS growth that are priced reasonably relative to their growth potential. Key secular themes informing our picks include the modernization of water infrastructure, localized thermal management in electric vehicles, specialized outsourced manufacturing in AI optics, and demographic tailwinds in healthcare services.

== 選股方法論 ==
我們的篩選方法論專注於尋找市值在 5 億至 500 億美元之間、在主流財經媒體和動能驅動的散戶交易關注之外營運的高品質中小型公司。我們系統性地過濾掉具有極端估值（本益比 > 30x 或本益成長比 > 1.5）、高負債權益比（> 1.0）或自由現金流為負的公司。相反地，我們優先考慮具有持久競爭護城河（如高轉換成本、專有技術或監管保護）和強大資產負債表的企業。我們透過選擇預估每股盈餘（EPS）持續保持雙位數成長，且其定價相對於其成長潛力合理的公司，來平衡成長與價值。影響我們選股的關鍵長期趨勢包括水資源基礎設施的現代化、電動車的局部熱管理、AI 光學領域的專業外包製造，以及醫療保健服務中的人口老化紅利。

== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
Our selection methodology focuses on identifying high-quality, mid-sized companies ($500M–$50B) that operate in niche markets with high barriers to entry, strong pricing power, and structural tailwinds. We systematically filtered out mega-caps, high-beta momentum stocks, and companies frequently hyped in mainstream media to avoid valuation bubbles. Instead, we prioritized businesses with a history of positive free cash flow, low leverage (Debt/Equity < 1.0 in most cases), and attractive PEG ratios (< 1.5). Key structural themes driving our selections include domestic manufacturing reshoring, infrastructure modernization, specialized healthcare demands, and mission-critical automation.

== 選股方法論 ==
我們的選股方法論專注於識別市值在中型區間（5億至500億美元）的高質量公司，這些公司在具有高准入門檻、強大定價權和結構性順風的利基市場中營運。我們系統性地過濾掉了超大市值股、高貝塔值的動能股以及在主流媒體中被頻繁炒作的公司，以避免估值泡沫。相反，我們優先考慮具有正自由現金流歷史、低槓桿（多數情況下負債權益比 < 1.0）以及具吸引力的本益成長比（PEG < 1.5）的企業。驅動我們選擇的核心結構性主題包括：國內製造業回流、基礎設施現代化、專業化醫療保健需求以及關鍵任務自動化。

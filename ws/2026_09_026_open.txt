== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening process was designed to systematically filter out high-flying, momentum-driven mega-caps and speculative retail favorites, focusing instead on high-quality, mid-sized compounders. First, we excluded any company with a market capitalization exceeding $50 billion or those currently experiencing extreme media sentiment. We then prioritized companies with a forward PEG ratio below 1.5, positive free cash flow yields, and conservative debt profiles (Debt/Equity < 1.2). The final selection was heavily influenced by structural macroeconomic tailwinds, including the reshoring of North American manufacturing, critical infrastructure modernization, demographic shifts in healthcare, and the non-GPU semiconductor supply chain. This balance of reasonable valuation and strong structural growth drivers provides a robust margin of safety for long-term investors.

== 選股方法論 ==
篩選過程旨在系統性地過濾掉高估值、動能驅動的巨型股和投機性的散戶熱門股，轉而關注高質量的中型複合增長股。首先，我們排除了市值超過 500 億美元的公司或目前媒體情緒極度高漲的公司。然後，我們優先考慮預估本益成長比 (PEG) 低於 1.5、自由現金流殖利率為正且債務結構保守（負債權益比 < 1.2）的公司。最終的選擇受到結構性宏觀經濟順風的強烈影響，包括北美製造業回流、關鍵基礎設施現代化、醫療保健領域的人口結構轉變以及非 GPU 半導體供應鏈。這種合理估值與強勁結構性增長驅動力的平衡，為長期投資者提供了強大的安全邊際。

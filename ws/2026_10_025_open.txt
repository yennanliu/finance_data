== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
This watchlist was constructed through fundamental screening logic aimed at identifying high-quality small-to-mid-cap compounders ($500M–$50B market cap) while eliminating viral momentum stocks, mega-cap tech, and market media darlings. Quantitative screens prioritized forward PEG ratios under 1.5, manageable debt-to-equity metrics, consistent positive free cash flow yields, and durable return on invested capital (ROIC). Qualitatively, the methodology selected businesses with structural competitive moats—such as irreplaceable physical assets, sole-source regulatory approvals, proprietary technological integration, or regional consolidation advantages. Macroeconomic themes informing these selections include industrial re-shoring, power grid infrastructure expansion, specialized biopharma manufacturing, smart municipal infrastructure, and non-discretionary essential service monopolies.

== 選股方法論 ==
本觀察名單透過基本面篩選邏輯建構，旨在找出優質的中小型複利成長股（市值約 5 億美元至 500 億美元），同時排除病毒式動能股、超大型科技股以及市場媒體寵兒。定量篩選優先考慮預估 PEG 低於 1.5、負債權益比可控、持續為正的自由現金流殖利率以及持久的投入資本報酬率（ROIC）。定性方面，本選股方法選擇了具有結構性競爭護城河的企業—例如不可替代的實體資產、獨家監管審批、專有技術整合或區域整合優勢。告知這些選擇的總體經濟主題包括工業製造業回流、電網基礎設施擴建、專門的生物製藥製造、智慧市政基礎設施以及非必需消費品之外的必要服務壟斷。

== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
Our selection methodology focuses on identifying high-quality, mid-and-small-cap compounders that operate away from the spotlight of mainstream financial media. We systematically screened out companies in the S&P 500 top-50 and those with excessive retail trading volume or speculative valuations (P/E > 30x or PEG > 1.5). We prioritized companies with strong balance sheets (low debt-to-equity), consistent free cash flow generation, and high returns on invested capital (ROIC). Growth and value were balanced by targeting businesses with double-digit EPS growth trajectories trading at reasonable valuations (PEG < 1.5). Key macro themes informing our picks include the reshoring of North American manufacturing, structural investments in infrastructure and grid modernization, energy efficiency regulations, and non-discretionary healthcare/plasma demand.

== 選股方法論 ==
我們的選股方法論專注於尋找不受主流財經媒體關注的高品質中小型複利型企業。我們系統性地篩選掉了標準普爾 500 指數前 50 大公司，以及散戶交易量過大或估值過高（本益比 > 30倍或本益成長比 PEG > 1.5）的公司。我們優先考慮資產負債表強健（低負債權益比）、持續產生自由現金流以及高投資資本回報率（ROIC）的公司。我們通過鎖定估值合理（PEG < 1.5）且每股盈餘（EPS）呈雙位數成長軌跡的企業，來平衡成長與價值。指導我們選股的主要總體經濟主題包括：北美製造業回流、基礎設施和電網現代化的結構性投資、節能法規，以及非自願性醫療保健/血漿需求。

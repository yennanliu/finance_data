== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
This watchlist was constructed using a quantitative and qualitative screening strategy aimed at discovering resilient, high-quality compounders outside mainstream market headlines. First, all S&P 500 top-50 mega-caps, high-beta momentum stocks, and retail viral names were excluded. Second, candidates were filtered for market capitalizations between $500M and $50B, low to moderate leverage (Debt/Equity < 1.2), and positive free cash flow yields. Third, we prioritized companies exhibiting clear competitive moats (e.g., proprietary tech platform, high switching costs, regulatory barriers, dominant niche distribution) and secular macro tailwinds (e.g., grid modernization, industrial automation, healthcare demographics, LTL consolidation). The final ranking heavily weighted the Price/Earnings-to-Growth (PEG) ratio and free cash flow generation to ensure an optimal balance between durable earnings trajectory and valuation safety margin.

== 選股方法論 ==
本觀察名單採用量化與質性篩選策略，旨在主流市場焦點之外尋找具備韌性的優質複利成長公司。首先，排除所有標普 500 前 50 大巨型股、高 Beta 衝量股及散戶熱門話題股。其次，候選公司需符合市值介於 5 億美元至 500 億美元之間、低至中等槓桿率（負債權益比 < 1.2）以及正自由現金流殖利率等條件。第三，我們優先選擇具備明確競爭護城河（如專利技術平台、高轉換成本、監管壁壘、主導性利基分銷）與長期宏觀順風（如電網現代化、工業自動化、醫療人口結構、零擔貨運整合）的公司。最終排名高度參考本益成長比（PEG）與自由現金流生成能力，以確保在持久的盈餘成長軌跡與估值安全邊際之間取得最佳平衡。

== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening process focused on identifying high-quality, mid-to-small-cap compounders that possess structural competitive advantages (moats) and trade at reasonable valuations relative to their growth prospects. To filter out volatile momentum and "meme" stocks, we excluded companies with extreme retail trading volumes, high short interest, or those frequently featured in mainstream retail trading forums. We prioritized businesses with high return on invested capital (ROIC), low leverage, and consistent free cash flow generation. Weighting was balanced between secular growth drivers (such as infrastructure modernization, automation, and defensive healthcare) and valuation discipline, targeting a PEG ratio under 1.5. This methodology ensures a portfolio of resilient, self-funding businesses capable of compounding capital through various macroeconomic environments.

== 選股方法論 ==
篩選過程側重於尋找具備結構性競爭優勢（護城河）且估值相對於其成長前景合理的優質中小型複合成長股。為了過濾掉高波動性的動能股和「迷因」股，我們排除了散戶交易量極端、空頭部位高或頻繁出現在主流散戶交易論壇中的公司。我們優先考慮具有高投入資本回報率（ROIC）、低槓桿和持續產生自由現金流的公司。在權衡長期成長驅動因素（如基礎設施現代化、自動化和防禦性醫療保健）與估值紀律之間取得了平衡，目標是將本益成長比（PEG）控制在 1.5 以下。這種方法論確保了投資組合由具備韌性、能自我融資且能在各種宏觀經濟環境中實現資本複合成長的企業組成。

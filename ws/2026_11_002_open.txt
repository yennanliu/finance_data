== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening framework applied to compile this 30-stock watchlist isolates under-the-radar, high-quality US businesses with market capitalizations predominantly between $500M and $50B, excluding all mega-cap tech giants and viral momentum stocks. Quantitative filters enforced reasonable relative valuations (target PEG < 1.5), manageable leverage (low Debt/Equity ratios), and cash-generative models (positive Free Cash Flow Yields). Qualitatively, the screening weighted structural tailwinds—such as electrification, data center buildouts, US manufacturing re-shoring, infrastructure modernization, and high-barrier healthcare technology—over short-term momentum. Stocks were ranked based on economic moat durability, financial health, return on invested capital, and reasonable forward valuation metrics.

== 選股方法論 ==
用於編制此 30 檔股票觀察名單的選股框架，旨在篩選出市值主要介於 5 億至 500 億美元之間、未被市場廣泛吹捧的優質美國企業，排除所有超大型科技巨頭和網路熱門炒作股。定量篩選條件要求合理的相對估值（目標本益成長比 PEG < 1.5）、可控的財務槓桿（低負債權益比）以及具備強勁現金產生能力的營運模式（正自由現金流殖利率）。在定性方面，篩選機制將結構性順風——例如電氣化、數據中心建設、美國製造業回流、基礎設施現代化和高壁壘醫療技術——置於短期動能之上。最終排名綜合考量了企業經濟護城河的持久性、財務健康狀況、投入資本回報率以及合理的遠期估值指標。

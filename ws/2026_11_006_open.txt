== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
This watchlist was constructed through a disciplined fundamental screening process targeting high-quality US equities with market capitalizations between $500M and $50B, intentionally excluding S&P 500 top-50 mega-caps, high-beta momentum stocks, and mainstream financial media darlings. The quantitative screener filtered for companies demonstrating positive forward earnings growth, manageable debt (low Debt/Equity or net cash positions), strong Free Cash Flow Yield, and attractive relative valuations (target PEG ratio < 1.5 where available). Qualitatively, priority was given to business models possessing identifiable competitive moats—such as high switching costs, regulatory mandates, high localized market share, or structural tailwinds like US industrial reshoring, infrastructure modernization, and grid transformation. Equity selections balance defensive recurring cash flow drivers with structural growth compounders to deliver durable long-term risk-adjusted returns.

== 選股方法論 ==
本觀察名單透過嚴格的基本面篩選程序建立，瞄準市值在 5 億美元至 500 億美元之間的優質美股，刻意排除 S&P 500 前 50 大巨型股、高 Beta 值的動能熱門股及主流財經媒體焦點。定量篩選標準包含：預估每股盈餘正成長、負債可控（低負債權益比或淨現金狀態）、強勁的自由現金流殖利率，以及具吸引力的相對估值（預估 PEG < 1.5 優先）。定性方面，優先選擇具備明確競爭護城河的商業模式—例如高轉換成本、法規強制需求、高區域市佔率，或受益於美國工業回流、基礎設施現代化與電網轉型等結構性順風。選股策略兼顧防守型經常性現金流與結構性複合成長，旨在提供持久的長期風險調整後回報。

== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening logic for this watchlist was designed to filter out the noise of the S&P 500 top-50 and high-beta momentum stocks, focusing instead on high-quality, under-the-radar compounders. We filtered for companies with market caps between $500M and $50B, requiring positive free cash flow, manageable debt-to-equity ratios, and a PEG ratio ideally under 1.5 to balance growth with valuation. The selection process heavily weighted Return on Invested Capital (ROIC) and structural competitive moats—such as high customer switching costs, regulatory barriers, or proprietary technology. Our macro thesis focused on resilient secular themes: domestic manufacturing reshoring, water infrastructure modernization, advanced semiconductor node enablement, and specialized B2B software, ensuring these companies possess multi-year tailwinds independent of short-term economic cycles.

== 選股方法論 ==
本觀察名單的篩選邏輯旨在過濾掉標普 500 指數前 50 大企業和高波動性動能股的雜音，轉而專注於優質、未被市場充分關注的複利型企業。我們篩選了市值在 5 億至 500 億美元之間的公司，要求其具備正向的自由現金流、可控的負債權益比，且本益成長比 (PEG) 理想情況下低於 1.5，以平衡成長性與估值。篩選過程高度看重投入資本回報率 (ROIC) 和結構性競爭護城河——例如高客戶轉換成本、監管壁壘或專有技術。我們的宏觀論點聚焦於具備韌性的長期趨勢：國內製造業回流、水資源基礎設施現代化、先進半導體節點支援以及專業 B2B 軟體，確保這些公司擁有獨立於短期經濟週期的多年期利多支持。

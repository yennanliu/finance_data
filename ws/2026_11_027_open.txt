== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
This watchlist was constructed through a multi-step screening process targeting US-listed small to mid-cap companies ($500M to $50B market cap) with high quality metrics and clear structural moats. We strictly excluded top-50 S&P 500 mega-caps, non-profitable speculative growth stocks, and stocks driven primarily by momentum or financial media coverage. Candidates were evaluated on forward EPS growth, free cash flow generation, manageable debt-to-equity ratios, and a preference for PEGs under 1.5. Macro and sector themes influencing this list include industrial re-shoring, water and grid infrastructure modernization, automated supply chain technology, specialized healthcare/bioprocessing, and specialized niche local monopolies. Weights were balanced evenly between consistent compounders with defensible market shares and deep-value growth opportunities exhibiting mispriced earnings trajectories.

== 選股方法論 ==
本關注名單係透過多步驟篩選程序所建立，旨在挑選具備高品質財務指標與明確結構性護城河的美股中小型企業（市值介於 5 億至 500 億美元之間）。我們嚴格排除標普 500 指數前 50 大巨型企業、未獲利的投機性成長股，以及主要由動能或主流財經媒體炒作所驅動的股票。候選公司依據預估每股盈餘成長、自由現金流流向、可控的負債權益比，以及本益成長比 (PEG) 偏好低於 1.5 等標準進行評估。影響此名單的宏觀與產業主題包括：工業製造業回流、水資源與電網基礎設施現代化、自動化供應鏈技術、專用醫療保健/生物加工，以及專業利基型的區域壟斷企業。權重在具有可防禦市場份額的穩定複利型公司與盈餘軌跡被錯估的深度價值成長機會之間取得平衡。

== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The selection methodology focused on identifying high-quality, under-the-radar US companies poised for long-term growth, steering clear of mega-caps, mainstream media darlings, and speculative momentum plays. I screened for companies with market caps between $500M and $50B, prioritizing those with consistent revenue and EPS growth trajectories, often with expanding margins or a clear path to improved profitability. A strong balance sheet, characterized by manageable debt and positive free cash flow, was a non-negotiable criterion. The core of the selection involved identifying companies with durable competitive moats—be it proprietary technology, strong brand equity, high switching costs, or essential services within an oligopolistic industry—and structural tailwinds from long-term trends like digital transformation, decarbonization, aging demographics, or infrastructure modernization. Value was assessed through a forward P/E and PEG ratio, with a preference for PEG below 1.5, though some higher-quality growth names with strong moats were included if their long-term prospects justified a higher multiple. Macro themes such as supply chain resilience, specialized software, sustainable infrastructure, and niche healthcare/consumer trends informed the sector diversity.

== 選股方法論 ==
本次選股方法論專注於識別高品質、被低估的美國公司，這些公司具備長期成長潛力，同時避免了巨型市值公司、主流媒體熱門股和投機性動能股。我篩選了市值介於5億美元至500億美元的公司，優先考慮那些營收和每股盈餘成長軌跡穩定、通常具有擴大毛利率或明確盈利改善路徑的公司。穩健的資產負債表，以可控的債務和正向的自由現金流為特徵，是一個不可協商的標準。選股的核心在於識別那些擁有持久競爭護城河——無論是專有技術、強大品牌資產、高轉換成本，還是寡頭壟斷行業中的基本服務——並受益於數位轉型、脫碳、人口老化或基礎設施現代化等長期趨勢所帶來的結構性順風的公司。價值評估透過預估本益比和本益成長比進行，偏好本益成長比低於1.5的公司，儘管一些具有強大護城河的優質成長股，如果其長期前景證明較高倍數合理，也被納入。供應鏈韌性、專業軟體、可持續基礎設施以及利基醫療保健/消費趨勢等宏觀主題為產業多元化提供了依據。

== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
Our screening process targeted US-listed companies with market capitalizations between $500 million and $50 billion, deliberately filtering out mega-caps (the top 50 in the S&P 500) and highly speculative, high-beta momentum stocks. We prioritized fundamental metrics, weighting consistent historical and forward-looking revenue and EPS growth alongside high free cash flow conversion and conservative debt levels. Our sector focus was informed by long-term structural tailwinds, including aging demographics, water scarcity, infrastructure modernization, and industrial automation. By selecting companies with high switching costs, regulatory barriers, or proprietary technologies, we identified resilient businesses capable of compounding capital over a multi-year horizon, independent of short-term macroeconomic volatility.

== 選股方法論 ==
我們的篩選流程鎖定了市值在 5 億美元至 500 億美元之間的美國上市公司，刻意過濾掉超大型股（標普 500 指數前 50 大企業）以及高投機性、高貝塔值的動能股。我們優先考慮基本面指標，將持續的歷史與預估營收及每股盈餘成長，與高自由現金流轉換率及保守的負債水平進行權衡。我們的產業焦點受到長期結構性順風的啟發，包括人口老化、水資源短缺、基礎設施現代化和工業自動化。藉由選擇具有高轉換成本、監管壁壘或專利技術的公司，我們識別出能夠在多年週期中實現資本複利增長的韌性企業，而不受短期總體經濟波動的影響。

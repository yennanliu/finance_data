== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
This watchlist was constructed using a quantitative and qualitative fundamental screening methodology designed to identify high-quality, under-the-radar US mid-cap compounders while avoiding speculative media darlings. First, the universe was filtered to remove S&P 500 top-50 mega-caps, high-beta meme stocks, and hyper-hyped tech names trading at speculative valuation multiples. Second, candidates were screened for fundamental strength: positive free cash flow yields, low to moderate financial leverage (Debt/Equity generally under 1.0), and attractive growth-adjusted valuations (PEG ratio targeted under 1.5). Third, qualitative analysis prioritized structural tailwinds—specifically industrial reshoring, grid modernization, data center thermal management, specialized healthcare niches, and commercial aerospace extension—where companies maintain verifiable economic moats such as sole-source contracts, regulatory switching costs, or localized scale monopolies.

== 選股方法論 ==
本觀察名單採用量化與質化相結合的基本面篩選方法論，旨在找出高品質、未被市場關注的美國中小型複合成長股，同時避開投機性的媒體熱門股。首先，篩選機制過濾掉 S&P 500 前 50 大巨型股、高 Beta 迷因股以及處於投機估值倍數的過熱科技股。其次，對候選公司進行基本面強度篩選：正向自由現金流殖利率、低至適度的財務槓桿（負債權益比通常低於 1.0），以及具吸引力的成長調整後估值（目標本益成長比 PEG 小於 1.5）。最後，質化分析優先考慮結構性順風——特別是工業回流、電網現代化、資料中心熱管理、專用醫療保健小眾市場及商業航太延展——在這些領域中，公司擁有可驗證的經濟護城河，例如獨家供應合約、監管轉換成本或區域性規模壟斷。

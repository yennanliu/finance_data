== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
This watchlist was generated using a strict fundamental equity screening framework focused on US-listed small- and mid-cap companies ($500M–$50B) possessing strong capital efficiency and structural competitive moats. High-beta momentum stocks, mega-cap tech giants, and retail media darlings were explicitly excluded. Screening parameters prioritized companies exhibiting double-digit forward EPS growth, manageable debt leverage (Debt/Equity < 0.8), positive free cash flow yields, and attractive valuation growth relative to earnings trajectory (PEG < 1.5 preferred). Selection logic heavily favors businesses aligned with durable long-term structural tailwinds—including US industrial reshoring, data center power and thermal infrastructure, grid modernization, specialty insurance pricing power, and specialized medical packaging—where fundamental operational execution drives stock returns independently of broader market headlines.

== 選股方法論 ==
本觀察名單採用嚴格的基本面篩選架構，聚焦於市值介於 5 億至 500 億美元之間、具備高資本效率與結構性競爭護城河的美國上市中小型企業。高 Beta 動量股、巨型科技巨頭及散戶熱門股已被明確排除。篩選參數優先考慮預估每股盈餘實現雙位數成長、負債槓桿可控（負債權益比 < 0.8）、自由現金流殖利率為正，且相較於盈餘成長軌跡具備吸引力估值（優先選擇本益成長比 PEG < 1.5）的公司。選股邏輯高度偏向契合長期結構性順風的企業——包括美國工業回流、資料中心電力與熱管理基礎設施、電網現代化、特種保險定價權及專業醫療包裝——在這些領域中，基本面的營運執行力是推動股票報酬的主因，而不受廣泛市場新聞頭條所干擾。

== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
This watchlist was constructed using a fundamental bottom-up screening process designed to isolate high-quality, long-term compounders while explicitly excluding mega-cap stocks, media-hyped momentum names, and speculative turnarounds. The universe was filtered to US-listed companies within the $500M to $50B market cap range that possess identifiable competitive moats (e.g., proprietary manufacturing technology, high switching costs, regulatory tailwinds). Stocks were weighted toward durable double-digit EPS growth trajectories, low to moderate debt loads (D/E < 1.0), positive free cash flow yields, and reasonable valuations (PEG ratio target < 1.5). Key macro secular themes informing these picks include US infrastructure modernization (IIJA), electrical grid expansion, AI data center liquid cooling demands, commercial aerospace supply ramps, and specialized healthcare delivery.

== 選股方法論 ==
本觀察名單採用自下而上的基本面篩選流程建立，旨在找出優質、具備長期複利能力的標的，同時明確排除巨型股（Mega-caps）、媒體炒作的順勢股以及投機性的轉型股。選股範圍限定於市值介於 5 億美元至 500 億美元之間、且擁有明確競爭護城河（例如專有製造技術、高昂的更換成本、法規利多）的美國上市公司。股票權重偏向持續實現雙位數 EPS 成長軌跡、低至中度負債負擔（D/E < 1.0）、正向自由現金流殖利率以及合理估值（本益成長比 PEG 目標 < 1.5）的公司。本觀察名單所依據的關鍵宏觀長期主題包括美國基礎設施現代化（IIJA）、電網擴建、AI 數據中心液冷需求、民航製造復甦以及特種醫療保健服務。

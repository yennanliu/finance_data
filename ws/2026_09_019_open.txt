== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening logic for this watchlist was designed to isolate high-quality, under-the-radar compounders while filtering out mega-cap tech giants and highly speculative meme/momentum stocks. We limited the universe to US-listed companies with market caps between $500M and $50B. To balance growth and value, we prioritized companies with consistent double-digit revenue and EPS growth trajectories, strong balance sheets (low debt-to-equity or net cash), and attractive valuations (targeting a PEG ratio under 1.5 where possible). We heavily weighted structural competitive moats—such as high switching costs, proprietary technology, and regulatory barriers—and aligned our picks with long-term macroeconomic themes, including the US infrastructure renaissance, water scarcity, cybersecurity compliance, aging demographics, and specialized industrial automation.

== 選股方法論 ==
本觀察名單的篩選邏輯旨在找出高質量、未受市場廣泛關注的複利型企業，同時排除標普 500 前 50 大科技巨頭以及投機性極強的迷因股/動能股。我們將選股範圍限制在市值介於 5 億至 500 億美元之間的美股上市公司。為了平衡成長與價值，我們優先考慮營收和每股盈餘（EPS）持續保持雙位數成長、資產負債表強勁（低負債權益比或淨現金），且估值具備吸引力（盡可能鎖定本益成長比 PEG 低於 1.5 的標的）的公司。我們高度重視結構性的競爭護城河（如高轉換成本、專有技術和監管壁壘），並使我們的選擇與長期宏觀經濟主題保持一致，包括美國基礎設施復興、水資源短缺、網絡安全合規、人口老齡化以及專業工業自動化。

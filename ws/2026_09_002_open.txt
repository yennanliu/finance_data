== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The selection methodology focused on identifying high-quality, mid-sized companies ($500M–$50B market cap) with durable competitive advantages and strong balance sheets, while systematically avoiding overhyped, high-valuation momentum stocks. We filtered out companies in the top-50 of the S&P 500 and those frequently featured in mainstream financial media to ensure a truly under-the-radar watchlist. Growth and value were balanced by prioritizing companies with a PEG ratio near or below 1.5, solid free cash flow yields, and low-to-moderate debt. Key structural themes driving our selections include infrastructure modernization, public safety technology, specialized healthcare diagnostics, and niche industrial reshoring.

== 選股方法論 ==
選股方法論專注於尋找具備持久競爭優勢和強健資產負債表的高品質中型企業（市值介於 5 億至 500 億美元之間），同時系統性地避開過度炒作、高估值的動能股。我們過濾掉了標普 500 指數前 50 大公司以及經常出現在主流財經媒體上的股票，以確保這是一份真正不顯山露水的觀察名單。我們通過優先考慮本益成長比（PEG）接近或低於 1.5、自由現金流殖利率穩健且負債率低至中等的公司，來平衡成長與價值。驅動我們選股的關鍵結構性主題包括基礎設施現代化、公共安全科技、專業醫療診斷以及利基工業回流。

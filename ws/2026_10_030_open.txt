== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
This watchlist was constructed by systematically filtering out mega-cap stocks ($50B+ market cap), highly volatile momentum/meme equities, and mainstream financial media darlings to focus strictly on under-the-radar fundamental compounders. The methodology screens for US-listed companies in the small-to-mid cap range ($500M to $50B) with strong balance sheets (typically low Debt/Equity < 0.8), positive free cash flow yields, and forward P/E ratios trading at a reasonable growth multiple (PEG < 1.5 preferred). Candidates were evaluated for identifiable competitive moats—such as proprietary technologies, municipal regulatory lock-ins, high switching costs, or local geographic monopolies—and structural macro tailwinds (e.g., power grid modernization, AI data center cooling infrastructure, US manufacturing reshoring, and aging demographics). The resulting watchlist balances growth and value, selecting high-return-on-capital businesses positioned for patient, multi-year compounding.

== 選股方法論 ==
本觀察名單透過系統化篩選，排除巨型股（市值 500 億美元以上）、高波動的動量/迷因股以及主流財經媒體熱炒的標的，專注於未被充分關注的基本面長期複利成長股。選股方法論聚焦於市值介於 5 億至 500 億美元的中小型美股，要求具備強健的資產負債表（通常負債權益比 < 0.8）、正向的自由現金流殖利率，以及相較於成長性估值合理的預估本益比（偏好 PEG < 1.5）。候選公司必須具備可識別的競爭護城河——例如專有技術、市政法規鎖定、高昂的替換成本或區域性地理寡占——以及結構性宏觀順風（如電網現代化、AI 資料中心液冷基礎設施、美國製造業回流及人口老齡化）。最終名單兼顧成長與價值，精選具備高投入資本回報率且適合長期耐心投資的優質企業。

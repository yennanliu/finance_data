== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening logic employed for this watchlist prioritized identifying high-quality businesses with durable competitive advantages and clear, long-term growth runways that are not yet widely recognized or overvalued by the broader market. To filter out "hot" stocks, I deliberately excluded companies frequently featured in mainstream financial media or those exhibiting characteristics of speculative momentum plays (e.g., extremely high P/E multiples without commensurate FCF, or reliance on single, unproven technologies). Instead, the focus was on established leaders in niche, essential industries or those benefiting from structural, multi-year tailwinds such as aging demographics, digital transformation (enterprise-focused, not consumer), infrastructure modernization, and supply chain resilience. Valuation played a critical role, with a strong preference for a forward P/E to growth (PEG) ratio below 1.5, indicating a reasonable price for the expected earnings trajectory. Companies with solid balance sheets (manageable debt, positive free cash flow or a clear path to it) were favored to ensure financial stability and flexibility. Macro themes like the continued demand for healthcare services, industrial automation, cybersecurity, and efficient resource management (water, energy) informed the sector allocation, seeking out companies that are enablers of these trends rather than speculative beneficiaries. The ranking reflects a blend of conviction in the moat's durability, the consistency and visibility of growth drivers, and the attractiveness of the valuation relative to that growth.

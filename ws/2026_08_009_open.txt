== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
Our screening methodology was designed to identify high-quality, resilient compounders while strictly avoiding the speculative excesses of the broader market. We filtered out any company with a market capitalization exceeding $50 billion or those frequently mentioned in mainstream financial media and social platforms, effectively eliminating overvalued mega-caps and momentum-driven 'meme' stocks. Instead, we focused on small-to-mid-cap companies ($500M to $50B) possessing strong balance sheets, positive free cash flow, and high returns on capital. We balanced growth and value by prioritizing companies with a PEG ratio below 1.5, ensuring we did not overpay for growth. Our selection was heavily informed by structural, multi-year macro themes, including domestic manufacturing reshoring, infrastructure modernization, automation to combat labor shortages, and energy-efficiency transitions. This disciplined approach highlights overlooked businesses with deep competitive moats that are well-positioned to deliver superior risk-adjusted returns over the long term.

== 選股方法論 ==
我們的篩選方法旨在識別高質量、具韌性的複合成長企業，同時嚴格避免大盤中的投機性過熱現象。我們過濾掉了任何市值超過 500 億美元的公司，以及在主流財經媒體和社群平台上頻繁被提及的公司，從而有效排除了估值過高的超大市值股和動能驅動的「迷因」股。相反，我們專注於市值在 5 億至 500 億美元之間、擁有健全資產負債表、正自由現金流和高資本回報率的中小市值公司。我們通過優先考慮本益成長比（PEG）低於 1.5 的公司來平衡成長與價值，確保我們不會為成長支付過高溢價。我們的選擇在很大程度上受到多年期結構性宏觀主題的啟發，包括國內製造業回流、基礎設施現代化、應對勞動力短缺的自動化以及節能轉型。這種紀律嚴明的方法突顯了那些擁有深厚競爭護城河、且在長期內有能力提供優異風險調整後回報的被忽視企業。

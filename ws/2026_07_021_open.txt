== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening logic focused on identifying high-quality, mid-cap companies ($500M to $50B) with durable competitive advantages and strong secular tailwinds, while systematically filtering out mega-cap tech giants, highly volatile momentum/meme stocks, and overhyped media darlings. We prioritized companies with a history of consistent revenue and EPS growth, robust free cash flow generation, and manageable debt profiles. Valuation was weighted heavily using the PEG ratio (targeting < 1.5) to ensure we are not overpaying for growth. Key macro themes informing our picks include the structural reshoring of US manufacturing, infrastructure modernization (water, power, and HVAC), demographic shifts driving healthcare demand, and the increasing adoption of automation and precision technology across industrial and supply chain sectors.

== 選股方法論 ==
篩選邏輯專注於尋找具有持久競爭優勢和強大長期趨勢的高質量中型企業（市值介於 5 億至 500 億美元之間），同時系統性地過濾掉大型科技巨頭、高波動性的動能/迷因股以及過度炒作的媒體寵兒。我們優先考慮具有持續營收和每股盈餘（EPS）成長歷史、強勁自由現金流生成能力以及可控債務狀況的公司。在估值方面，我們高度重視本益成長比（PEG，目標小於 1.5），以確保我們沒有為成長支付過高溢價。影響我們選股的關鍵宏觀主題包括美國製造業的結構性回流、基礎設施現代化（水務、電力和 HVAC）、推動醫療保健需求的人口結構變化，以及工業和供應鏈領域對自動化和精準技術的日益採用。

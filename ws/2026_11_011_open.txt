== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
This watchlist was generated using a fundamental screening model tailored for long-term equity growth without taking on excessive valuation risk or momentum exposure. The process systematically excluded all mega-cap technology firms (e.g., S&P 500 top 50) and trending viral retail stocks to eliminate media hype bias. Companies were filtered for market capitalization between $500M and $50B, prioritizing small- to mid-cap businesses benefiting from structural real-economy tailwinds—such as power grid modernization, AI data center cooling infrastructure, North American industrial reshoring, and specialized automation. Stocks were weighted by balance sheet strength (low debt-to-equity and positive free cash flow yields) combined with sensible valuations (preferring a PEG ratio below 1.5). Every selected firm possesses an identifiable competitive moat, high customer switching costs, or sticky recurring revenue drivers.

== 選股方法論 ==
本觀察名單係採用基本面篩選模型所生成，專注於長期的股票成長，同時避免承擔過高的估值風險或動量追漲風險。篩選過程系統性地排除了所有超大型科技公司（如 S&P 500 前 50 大企業）以及熱門網路話題股，以消除媒體炒作偏誤。入選公司的市值均介於 5 億美元至 500 億美元之間，優先選擇受益於實體經濟結構性順風的中小型企業——例如電網升級、AI 數據中心液冷基礎設施、北美工業回流以及專業自動化。選股指標著重於資產負債表的健診度（低負債權益比與良好的自由現金流殖利率）以及合理的估值（優先選擇本益成長比 PEG 低於 1.5 的標的）。每一家獲選的公司均具備明確的競爭護城河、極高的客戶轉換成本或強勁的經常性收入驅動因素。

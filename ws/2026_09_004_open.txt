== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
Our screening process focused on identifying high-quality, mid-cap compounders ($500M to $50B market cap) that possess durable competitive advantages and trade at reasonable valuations relative to their growth prospects (PEG < 1.5). We systematically excluded mega-cap stocks, high-beta momentum names, and companies frequently discussed in retail trading forums or mainstream media. Instead, we prioritized businesses with high returns on invested capital (ROIC), low leverage (debt-to-equity typically under 0.5), and robust free cash flow generation. Key secular themes driving our selections include domestic manufacturing reshoring, infrastructure modernization (water and electrical), advanced semiconductor packaging, and demographic-driven healthcare services. This value-disciplined, growth-oriented framework ensures a portfolio of resilient businesses capable of compounding capital over a multi-year horizon.

== 選股方法論 ==
我們的篩選流程專注於尋找高質量、中型市值的複利型企業（市值介於 5 億至 500 億美元之間），這些企業擁有持久的競爭優勢，且交易價格相對於其成長前景而言非常合理（本益成長比 PEG < 1.5）。我們系統性地排除了超大市值股票、高貝塔值的動能股，以及在散戶交易論壇或主流媒體中被頻繁討論的公司。相反地，我們優先考慮具有高投入資本回報率（ROIC）、低槓桿（負債權益比通常低於 0.5）以及強勁自由現金流生成能力的企業。推動我們選股的關鍵長期主題包括：國內製造業回流、基礎設施現代化（水務與電氣）、先進半導體封裝以及人口結構驅動的醫療保健服務。這種紀律嚴明的價值與成長並重框架，確保了投資組合由能夠在多年週期中實現資本複利的韌性企業所組成。

== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening framework prioritizes fundamental compounders operating within high-barrier-to-entry niches while strictly excluding mega-cap momentum or media-hyped equities. Companies were filtered to ensure market capitalizations fall within the small/mid-cap spectrum ($500M to $50B) with forward PEG ratios generally beneath 1.5x, solid debt-to-equity metrics, and positive free cash flow profiles. The weighting emphasizes sustainable organic revenue growth, margin expansion potential, and structural macro tailwinds—such as infrastructure modernization, industrial reshoring/automation, specialized healthcare delivery, and grid electrification—over short-term momentum.

== 選股方法論 ==
本選股篩選框架優先考量在高度進入壁壘領域營運的基本面複合成長型企業，嚴格排除巨型股、動量熱門股或媒體炒作個股。篩選條件確保市值落在中小型股範圍（50 億至 500 億美元之間），預估本益成長比（PEG）普遍低於 1.5 倍，具備健全的負債權益比與正向自由現金流狀況。相較於短期動能，本權重設計更看重可持續的有機營收成長、利潤率擴張潛力以及結構性宏觀順風——例如基礎設施現代化、工業製造回流/自動化、特化醫療服務以及電網電氣化。

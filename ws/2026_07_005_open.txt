== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening process prioritized companies exhibiting a clear, durable competitive advantage or structural tailwind, ensuring long-term relevance regardless of short-term market noise. To avoid "market media darlings," I focused on established leaders in niche or specialized segments rather than broad consumer tech or highly speculative sectors. Quantitative filters emphasized strong revenue and EPS growth trajectories, with a preference for PEG ratios below 1.5, indicating growth at a reasonable price. A solid balance sheet, characterized by manageable debt and positive free cash flow, was non-negotiable for financial resilience. Macro themes like digital transformation (AI infrastructure, cloud enablement, specialized software), healthcare innovation (med-tech, diagnostics), clean energy transition, and infrastructure modernization heavily influenced the sector allocation, identifying companies that are essential enablers rather than just beneficiaries.

== 選股方法論 ==
選股過程優先考慮那些具有明確、持久競爭優勢或結構性順風的公司，確保其長期相關性不受短期市場噪音影響。為避免「市場媒體寵兒」，我專注於利基或專業領域的成熟領導者，而非廣泛的消費科技或高度投機性行業。量化篩選側重於強勁的營收和每股盈餘增長軌跡，並偏好本益成長比 (PEG) 低於 1.5 的公司，這表明以合理價格實現增長。穩健的資產負債表，以可控債務和正自由現金流為特徵，是財務彈性的必要條件。宏觀主題，如數位轉型（AI 基礎設施、雲端賦能、專業軟體）、醫療保健創新（醫療技術、診斷）、清潔能源轉型和基礎設施現代化，極大地影響了行業分配，識別出那些作為關鍵推動者而非僅僅受益者的公司。

== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening framework for this watchlist specifically excluded mega-cap technology leaders, viral momentum stocks, and financial media darlings to focus on under-the-radar fundamental compounders. The universe was filtered to U.S.-listed equities with market capitalizations primarily between $500M and $50B, requiring positive free cash flow, debt-to-equity ratios below 1.0 (with minor exceptions for highly cash-generative models), and forward PEG ratios generally below 1.5. Growth and value were balanced by prioritizing companies exhibiting high return on invested capital (ROIC) and pricing power driven by secular tailwinds—such as power grid modernization, data center thermal management, automated manufacturing, and specialty insurance. Preference was assigned to niche market leaders boasting strong balance sheets that can continuously compound capital through economic cycles without depending on speculative market liquidity.

== 選股方法論 ==
本觀察名單的篩選框架特別排除巨型科技龍頭股、熱門動能股及主流財經媒體熱議焦點，專注於尚未被充分發掘的基本面複合成長股。選股池過濾為市值主要介於 5 億美元至 500 億美元之間的美國上市公司，要求具備正向自由現金流、負債權益比低於 1.0（少數具備強勁現金產生能力的模型例外），且預估本益成長比（PEG）普遍低於 1.5。在成長與價值之間取得平衡的方式，是優先選擇具備高投入資本回報率（ROIC）及由結構性順風推動定價能力的公司——例如電網現代化、資料中心熱管理、自動化製造及特種保險。偏好具備強健資產負債表的細分市場領導者，使其能在經濟週期中持續複合累積資本，而不依賴投機性的市場流動性。

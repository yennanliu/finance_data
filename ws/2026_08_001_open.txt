== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
Our screening methodology focuses on identifying high-quality, mid-and-small-cap compounders that operate outside the mainstream spotlight. We filtered out any companies in the S&P 500 top-50 or those with excessive media hype to avoid valuation bubbles. We prioritized businesses with strong balance sheets (low debt-to-equity and robust free cash flow), high barriers to entry (regulatory, technological, or geographic moats), and secular tailwinds such as infrastructure modernization, advanced semiconductor packaging, and demographic shifts. Growth and valuation were balanced by targeting a forward PEG ratio ideally below 1.5, ensuring we do not overpay for growth.

== 選股方法論 ==
我們的選股方法論專注於尋找運營於主流關注之外的高品質中小型複合成長股。我們過濾掉了標普500前50大公司以及過度被媒體炒作的企業，以避免估值泡沫。我們優先考慮具有強勁資產負債表（低負債權益比和穩健的自由現金流）、高進入壁壘（法規、技術或地理護城河）以及順應長期趨勢（如基礎設施現代化、先進半導體封裝和人口結構變化）的企業。我們通過將預估本益成長比（PEG）控制在 1.5 以下來平衡成長性與估值，以確保我們不會為成長支付過高的代價。

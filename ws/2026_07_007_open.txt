== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening process for this watchlist prioritized identifying fundamentally strong, under-the-radar US companies with clear long-term growth catalysts, while strictly avoiding mega-caps, media darlings, or momentum plays. Initial filters excluded any company in the S&P 500 top-50 by market cap, and any stock frequently appearing on mainstream "hot lists" or exhibiting meme-like trading characteristics. The sweet spot for market capitalization was set between $500M and $50B to ensure a focus on small to mid-cap opportunities. Companies were then evaluated based on robust financial health, including consistent double-digit revenue and EPS growth trajectories, a clear path to positive free cash flow (or strong existing FCF yields), and manageable debt levels (Debt/Equity generally below 1.0). Valuation was a key differentiator, with a preference for PEG ratios below 1.5, indicating reasonable growth-adjusted pricing, though some high-quality, sticky software businesses were allowed slightly higher PEG if their moats and growth runways were exceptionally strong. Finally, each selected company had to possess a discernible competitive moat (e.g., high switching costs, network effects, proprietary technology, regulatory barriers) or benefit from powerful structural tailwinds such such as an aging population, digital transformation, supply chain resilience, energy efficiency, or specialized healthcare demands. This balanced approach of growth, value, and fundamental strength across diverse sectors informed the final ranking.

== 選股方法論 ==
本次選股流程優先識別基本面強勁、尚未被市場充分發掘的美國公司，這些公司具有明確的長期成長催化劑，同時嚴格避免市值過大的巨頭、媒體熱門股或動能股。初步篩選排除了任何市值排名前50的標普500指數成分股，以及任何經常出現在主流「熱門榜單」或表現出迷因式交易特徵的股票。市值範圍設定在5億美元至500億美元之間，以確保專注於中小型股的機會。隨後，根據穩健的財務狀況對公司進行評估，包括持續兩位數的營收和每股盈餘成長軌跡、明確的自由現金流正向路徑（或強勁的現有自由現金流殖利率），以及可控的債務水平（負債權益比通常低於1.0）。估值是關鍵的區分因素，偏好本益成長比（PEG）低於1.5的公司，這表明其成長調整後的定價合理，儘管對於一些高品質、客戶黏性高的軟體公司，如果其護城河和成長空間特別強勁，則允許略高的PEG。最後，每個入選公司都必須具備可識別的競爭護城河（例如高轉換成本、網路效應、專有技術、監管壁壘）或受惠於強大的結構性順風，例如人口老化、數位轉型、供應鏈韌性、能源效率或專業醫療保健需求。這種在多元化產業中平衡成長、價值和基本面實力的綜合方法，構成了最終排名的基礎。

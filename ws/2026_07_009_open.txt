== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The selection process focused on identifying US-listed companies with robust, long-term fundamental growth drivers that are not currently in the market's spotlight. I filtered out mega-caps and any stock frequently appearing on "hot lists" or exhibiting meme-like trading characteristics, prioritizing businesses with established operations and clear competitive advantages. Emphasis was placed on companies benefiting from structural tailwinds such such as aging demographics, infrastructure modernization, industrial automation, water scarcity, and specialized technology adoption, rather than speculative trends. Valuation was a key consideration, favoring companies with reasonable P/E ratios relative to their forward EPS growth (PEG generally below 1.5, though some high-quality compounders might exceed this slightly due to strong moats). A solid balance sheet with manageable debt and consistent free cash flow generation was non-negotiable, ensuring financial resilience and self-funded growth. The final ranked list reflects a balance of growth potential, moat strength, and valuation attractiveness across diverse, often overlooked, sectors.

== 選股方法論 ==
本次選股過程著重於識別那些具有強勁、長期基本面成長動力，但目前並非市場焦點的美國上市公司。我篩選掉了大型股以及任何頻繁出現在「熱門榜單」或呈現迷因股交易特徵的股票，優先考慮那些營運成熟且具有明確競爭優勢的企業。重點放在受益於結構性趨勢的公司，例如人口老化、基礎設施現代化、工業自動化、水資源短缺和專業技術採用，而非投機性趨勢。估值是一個關鍵考量，偏好那些預期每股盈餘成長（PEG通常低於1.5，儘管一些高品質的複合型成長股由於其強大的護城河可能略高於此）相對合理的本益比公司。穩健的資產負債表、可控的債務和持續的自由現金流產生是不可或缺的，以確保財務韌性和自我資金成長。最終的排名清單平衡了成長潛力、護城河強度和估值吸引力，涵蓋了多元化且常被忽視的產業。

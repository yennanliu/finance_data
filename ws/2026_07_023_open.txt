== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
Our screening process deliberately filters out mega-cap tech giants and momentum-driven media favorites by excluding companies in the top-50 of the S&P 500 and those trading at speculative, hype-driven valuations. Instead, we focus on a "Growth At A Reasonable Price" (GARP) framework, prioritizing companies with a PEG ratio ideally under 1.5, robust free cash flow yields, and solid balance sheets with manageable debt. We heavily weight structural, multi-decade macro tailwinds—such as industrial reshoring, infrastructure modernization, water scarcity, automation, and demographic aging—combined with micro-level competitive moats like high switching costs, proprietary technology, or dominant niche market shares. This disciplined approach uncovers resilient, under-the-radar compounders poised for long-term outperformance.

== 選股方法論 ==
我們的篩選流程刻意過濾了大型科技巨頭和動能驅動的媒體寵兒，排除標普 500 指數前 50 大公司以及交易價格處於投機、炒作估值的企業。相反地，我們專注於「合理價格成長」（GARP）框架，優先考慮本益成長比（PEG）理想情況下低於 1.5、自由現金流殖利率強健且負債可控、資產負債表穩健的公司。我們高度重視結構性、跨越數十年的總體經濟順風——例如製造業回流、基礎設施現代化、水資源短缺、自動化和人口老化——並結合微觀層面的競爭護城河，如高轉換成本、專利技術或主導性的利基市場份額。這種紀律嚴明的方法發掘了具有韌性、不顯眼的複合成長股，有望實現長期的超額回報。

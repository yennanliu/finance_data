== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening process was designed to filter out mega-cap stocks, speculative meme stocks, and overhyped media favorites, focusing instead on high-quality mid-cap companies ($500M to $50B) with durable competitive advantages. We prioritized companies with a PEG ratio below 1.5, low debt-to-equity ratios, and strong, consistent free cash flow yields. The selection was heavily informed by long-term structural macro trends, including the reshoring of US manufacturing, infrastructure modernization (grid, water, and transit), automation in response to labor shortages, and secular demographic shifts (aging population). Growth and value were balanced by targeting companies that dominate their respective niches but trade at reasonable valuations relative to their forward earnings trajectories.

== 選股方法論 ==
本篩選流程旨在排除市值前50大的超級大企業、投機性迷因股以及過度炒作的媒體寵兒，轉而專注於在各自利基市場中擁有持久競爭優勢的高品質中型股（市值介於5億至500億美元之間）。我們優先考慮本益成長比（PEG）低於1.5、負債權益比低且自由現金流殖利率強勁且穩定的公司。選股決策深受長期結構性宏觀趨勢的啟發，包括美國製造業回流、基礎設施現代化（電網、水利和交通）、應對勞動力短缺的自動化升級，以及長期的結構性人口結構變化（人口老化）。我們在成長與價值之間取得平衡，鎖定那些在各自領域佔據主導地位、但相對於其預估盈餘軌跡而言估值合理的企業。

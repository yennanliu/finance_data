== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening process designed for this watchlist strictly filters out mega-cap companies and high-momentum "meme" stocks, focusing instead on small-to-mid-cap businesses ($500M to $50B) with durable competitive advantages and robust balance sheets. Growth and valuation are balanced by prioritizing companies with reasonable PEG ratios or highly defensive, recurring cash flows. Key secular themes guiding these selections include infrastructure modernization (smart water meters, HVAC systems), specialized software with high switching costs (government and supply chain software), and recession-resistant industrial niches (E&S insurance, aerospace aftermarket parts, and regional waste management). This ensures the portfolio is positioned to compound capital steadily across varying macroeconomic environments.

== 選股方法論 ==
本篩選邏輯旨在排除市值前 50 大的巨型企業以及高動能、迷因/熱門概念股，專注於尋找具備持久競爭護城河、高資本回報率和強健資產負債表（低負債、高現金流轉換率）的優質中小市值企業（市值介於 5 億至 500 億美元之間）。我們特別看重能受益於結構性順風（如基礎設施升級、水資源稀缺、特殊險市場硬化、專用軟體普及、航空航天售後市場）的行業。在成長性與估值之間進行權衡時，我們優先考慮本益成長比（PEG）合理，或擁有高度防禦性、經常性收入來源的標的，以確保在長期投資中獲得穩健的複利回報。

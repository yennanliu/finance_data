== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening logic utilized to generate this ranked watchlist focused strictly on identifying high-quality, under-the-radar compounders while filtering out mega-cap tech and speculative momentum stocks. First, the universe was restricted to US-listed equities with market caps between $500M and $50B, eliminating both highly volatile micro-caps and over-analyzed mega-caps. Second, to filter out "media darlings" and valuation bubbles, we excluded companies with trailing P/E ratios over 30x unless justified by exceptional, highly visible growth, prioritizing a PEG ratio of under 1.5. Growth and value were balanced by selecting companies with proven, positive free cash flow (FCF) yields and manageable debt-to-equity ratios. The selection was heavily informed by structural macro themes, including the domestic manufacturing renaissance (reshoring), grid modernization, data center infrastructure expansion, water scarcity, and the automation of labor-starved industries. This fundamental approach ensures each company possesses a durable competitive moat and a clear path to compounding shareholder value over a multi-year horizon.

== 選股方法論 ==
用於生成此排名觀察名單的篩選邏輯，嚴格專注於識別高質量、未受市場廣泛關注的複利型企業，同時過濾掉超大市值科技股和投機性動能股。首先，將選股範圍限制在市值介於 5 億美元至 500 億美元之間的美國上市公司，從而消除了高波動性的微型股和已被過度分析的超大型股。其次，為了過濾掉「媒體寵兒」和估值泡沫，我們排除了滾動本益比超過 30 倍的公司（除非有極其卓越且高可見度的成長支持），並優先考慮本益成長比（PEG）低於 1.5 的個股。在成長與價值之間取得平衡，篩選出具有經證實、正向自由現金流（FCF）殖利率且負債權益比可控的公司。選股決策深受結構性宏觀主題的啟發，包括國內製造業復興（回流）、電網現代化、數據中心基礎設施擴展、水資源短缺以及勞動力短缺行業的自動化。這種基本面方法確保了每家公司都擁有持久的競爭護城河，以及在多年週期內持續複利股東價值的清晰路徑。

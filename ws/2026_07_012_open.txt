== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening logic focused on identifying high-quality, under-the-radar US-listed companies with sustainable long-term growth prospects, avoiding the noise of mega-caps and media darlings. First, a broad universe of US stocks was filtered to exclude companies in the S&P 500 top-50 by market cap and those frequently featured in mainstream financial news or exhibiting meme/momentum characteristics (e.g., extremely high P/S, speculative narratives). The remaining candidates were then assessed for fundamental strength: consistent revenue and EPS growth trajectories, expanding margins, and a compelling valuation (PEG < 1.5 preferred, though slightly higher was accepted for high-quality moats). A robust balance sheet with manageable debt and positive free cash flow (or a clear path to it) was a non-negotiable criterion. Finally, a strong competitive moat (e.g., high switching costs, proprietary technology, network effects) or a clear structural tailwind (e.g., AI adoption, healthcare innovation, supply chain resilience, water scarcity) was essential. The sweet spot for market cap was $500M-$50B, targeting small to mid-cap leaders poised for continued expansion. Macro themes like digital transformation, industrial automation, specialized healthcare, and sustainable infrastructure heavily informed the sector allocation.

== 選股方法論 ==
選股邏輯側重於識別具有可持續長期成長前景的高品質、不為人知的美國上市公司，同時避免大型股和媒體寵兒的噪音。首先，對廣泛的美國股票池進行篩選，排除市值排名前 50 的標普 500 公司，以及那些經常出現在主流財經新聞中或表現出迷因/動量特徵（例如，極高的市銷率、投機性敘事）的公司。然後，對剩餘的候選公司進行基本面評估：穩定的營收和每股盈餘成長軌跡、不斷擴大的利潤率，以及具有吸引力的估值（本益成長比優選小於 1.5，但對於具有高品質護城河的公司，略高也可接受）。擁有可控債務和正自由現金流（或明確的實現路徑）的穩健資產負債表是不可協商的標準。最後，強大的競爭護城河（例如，高轉換成本、專有技術、網路效應）或明確的結構性順風（例如，AI 採用、醫療保健創新、供應鏈韌性、水資源短缺）至關重要。市值的最佳區間為 5 億美元至 500 億美元，目標是中小型市值領導者，這些公司有望持續擴張。數位轉型、工業自動化、專業醫療保健和永續基礎設施等宏觀主題在很大程度上影響了行業分配。

== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening process for this watchlist focused on identifying high-quality, mid-cap companies ($500M–$50B) that possess strong competitive advantages (moats) and secular tailwinds but are ignored by mainstream media and retail momentum. We filtered out mega-caps and high-beta "meme" stocks to prioritize fundamental compounders. Our quantitative filters required consistent revenue and EPS growth, expanding operating margins, manageable debt (Debt/Equity < 1.0 in most cases), and attractive valuation relative to growth (PEG < 1.5). Qualitatively, we favored companies operating in niche markets with high switching costs, regulatory barriers, or structural tailwinds—such as water infrastructure, specialized software, advanced industrial automation, and niche healthcare services. This balance of growth and valuation ensures a margin of safety for long-term investors.

== 選股方法論 ==
本觀察名單的篩選過程專注於尋找具備強大競爭優勢（護城河）和長期結構性利多，但被主流媒體和散戶動能忽視的高品質中型企業（市值介於 5 億至 500 億美元之間）。我們過濾掉了超大型股和高波動的「迷因」股，以優先考慮基本面穩健的複利型企業。在定量篩選上，我們要求具備持續的營收和每股盈餘成長、營業利益率擴張、可控的債務（多數情況下負債權益比低於 1.0），以及相較於成長性具吸引力的估值（本益成長比 PEG 低於 1.5）。在定性篩選上，我們偏好在利基市場中營運、擁有高轉換成本、監管壁壘或結構性利多的公司，例如水務基礎設施、專業軟體、先進工業自動化和利基醫療服務。這種成長性與估值的平衡為長期投資者確保了安全邊際。

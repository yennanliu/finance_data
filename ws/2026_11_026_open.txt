== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening process prioritizes quality long-term compounders while explicitly excluding S&P 500 mega-caps, high-profile headline stocks, and momentum/meme equities. Quantitative filters required market caps between $500M and $50B, forward PEG ratios under 1.5 (or deeply depressed P/E with high double-digit FCF yields), conservative debt-to-equity ratios (<1.2x), and positive free cash flow metrics. Qualitative screens focused on durable competitive moats—such as high customer switching costs, regulatory tailwinds, proprietary technology, or structural cost advantages—and secular macro trends including data center power infrastructure, grid modernization, healthcare efficiency, advanced semiconductor packaging, and industrial reshoring.

== 選股方法論 ==
本選股篩選流程優先考慮具備長期優質複利成長能力的個股，同時嚴格排除 S&P 500 超大型市值股、高熱度新聞頭條焦點股以及動量/迷因股。量化篩選指標要求市值介於 5 億美元至 500 億美元之間、預估本益成長比（PEG）低於 1.5（或本益比極低且自由現金流殖利率高達雙位數）、保守的負債權益比（<1.2x）以及正向的自由現金流指標。質化篩選則專注於持久的競爭護城河——例如高客戶切換成本、法規政策順風、專利技術或結構性成本優勢——以及長期宏觀大趨勢，包含資料中心電力基礎設施、電網現代化、醫療效率提升、先進半導體分裝與工業製造業回流。

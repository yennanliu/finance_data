== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening process for this watchlist was designed to filter out highly speculative, overhyped, and overvalued equities, focusing instead on high-quality, under-the-radar compounders. First, we excluded all companies in the S&P 500 top-50 by market cap and eliminated stocks with elevated social media sentiment or extreme momentum indicators to avoid "meme" dynamics. We then applied strict fundamental filters: positive free cash flow, manageable leverage (Debt/Equity < 1.5, with a preference for net cash balance sheets), and a forward PEG ratio ideally below 1.5. The final selection was weighted toward companies with clear structural tailwinds—such as US manufacturing reshoring, infrastructure spending, climate-resilient building codes, and demographic shifts—combined with strong competitive moats like high switching costs, regulatory barriers, or localized monopolies.

== 選股方法論 ==
本觀察名單的篩選過程旨在過濾掉高投機性、過度炒作和估值過高的股票，轉而關注高質量、不被市場關注的複利型企業。首先，我們排除了市值前 50 名的標普 500 指數成分股，並剔除了社交媒體熱度高或具有極端動能指標的股票，以避免「迷因」效應。接著，我們應用了嚴格的基本面篩選：正的自由現金流、可控的槓桿率（負債權益比 < 1.5，並偏好淨現金資產負債表），以及預估本益成長比（PEG）理想情況下低於 1.5。最終的選擇偏向於那些擁有明確結構性順風（如美國製造業回流、基礎設施支出、氣候適應型建築規範和人口結構轉變）並結合強大競爭護城河（如高轉換成本、監管壁壘或區域壟斷）的公司。

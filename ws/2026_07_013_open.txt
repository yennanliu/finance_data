== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
My screening methodology focused on identifying high-quality businesses with durable competitive advantages and strong, yet often overlooked, growth trajectories. I first filtered out mega-caps and any company frequently appearing in mainstream financial headlines or retail trading forums, ensuring a focus on truly under-the-radar opportunities. The core of the selection involved a deep dive into fundamental growth drivers, prioritizing companies with consistent double-digit revenue and EPS growth, or those demonstrating a clear path to expanding margins. A strong balance sheet with manageable debt and positive free cash flow was non-negotiable, indicating financial resilience. Crucially, each pick possesses an identifiable competitive moat—be it network effects, high switching costs, proprietary technology, regulatory advantages, or essential infrastructure—which underpins long-term pricing power and market share. Valuation was considered through a PEG ratio lens, favoring companies with a PEG below 1.5 where possible, or accepting slightly higher multiples for businesses with exceptional moats and growth visibility. Macro themes like digital transformation, infrastructure modernization, clean energy transition, precision healthcare, and automation informed sector selection, ensuring picks benefit from multi-decade tailwinds.

== 選股方法論 ==
我的篩選方法著重於識別具有持久競爭優勢和強勁但常被忽視的增長軌跡的優質企業。我首先排除了大型股以及任何經常出現在主流金融新聞頭條或散戶交易論壇的公司，以確保專注於真正不為人知的機會。選股的核心是對基本增長驅動因素進行深入分析，優先考慮那些具有持續兩位數營收和每股盈餘增長的公司，或那些明確顯示出利潤率擴大趨勢的公司。擁有可控債務和正自由現金流的穩健資產負債表是不可或缺的，這表明了財務韌性。至關重要的是，每個選股都擁有可識別的競爭護城河——無論是網路效應、高轉換成本、專有技術、監管優勢還是關鍵基礎設施——這支撐了長期的定價能力和市場份額。估值透過本益成長比（PEG）進行考量，盡可能偏好PEG低於1.5的公司，或對於具有卓越護城河和增長可見性的企業接受略高的倍數。數位轉型、基礎設施現代化、清潔能源轉型、精準醫療和自動化等宏觀主題為行業選擇提供了依據，確保所選股票受益於數十年的長期趨勢。

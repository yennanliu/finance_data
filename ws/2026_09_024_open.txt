== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening logic for this watchlist was designed to filter out mega-cap market drivers and highly volatile momentum/meme stocks, focusing instead on high-quality compounders in the small-to-mid-cap space ($500M–$50B). We prioritized companies with strong balance sheets (low debt-to-equity ratios and robust free cash flow yields) and reasonable valuations relative to their growth trajectories (preferring a PEG ratio below 1.5). Weight was heavily given to businesses with clear structural tailwinds—such as the reshoring of US manufacturing, infrastructure modernization, water conservation, and the electrification of industrial systems. By targeting companies with niche dominant market shares or regulatory-driven moats, this methodology uncovers resilient businesses capable of compounding capital over the long term, independent of broader macroeconomic noise.

== 選股方法論 ==
本觀察名單的篩選邏輯旨在過濾掉超大市值的市場風向標以及高波動性的動能/迷因股，轉而專注於中小市值區間（5億至500億美元）的高品質複利企業。我們優先考慮資產負債表強健（低負債權益比和強勁的自由現金流殖利率）且估值相對於其成長軌跡合理（傾向於本益成長比 PEG 低於 1.5）的公司。選股權重高度向具有明確結構性順風的企業傾斜——例如美國製造業回流、基礎設施現代化、水資源保護以及工業系統電氣化。通過鎖定具有利基市場主導份額或受法規驅動護城河的公司，本方法論發掘出了能夠在長期內實現資本複利成長的具韌性企業，而不受更廣泛的宏觀經濟雜音干擾。

== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening logic used to construct this watchlist focused on filtering out highly speculative, over-hyped, and overvalued companies, while prioritizing high-quality compounders with durable competitive advantages. First, we excluded any company with a market capitalization exceeding $50 billion or those frequently featured in mainstream momentum-driven financial media. Next, we applied a strict fundamental filter: requiring positive free cash flow (or a clear near-term path to it), manageable debt-to-equity ratios, and a forward PEG ratio ideally below 1.5 to ensure growth is purchased at a reasonable price. We heavily weighted return on invested capital (ROIC) and pricing power, favoring companies with high switching costs, regulatory tailwinds, or network effects. The selection is anchored in secular macro themes, including industrial reshoring, infrastructure modernization, demographic aging, and the rising demand for energy efficiency.

== 選股方法論 ==
構建此觀察名單的篩選邏輯側重於過濾掉高度投機、過度炒作和估值過高的公司，同時優先選擇具有持久競爭優勢的高質量複利型企業。首先，我們排除了任何市值超過 500 億美元或經常出現在主流動能驅動型財經媒體上的公司。接著，我們應用了嚴格的基本面篩選：要求具備正的自由現金流（或明確的近期實現路徑）、可控的負債權益比，以及預估本益成長比（PEG）理想情況下低於 1.5，以確保以合理的價格購買成長。我們高度重視投入資本回報率（ROIC）和定價權，偏好具有高轉換成本、法規利多或網路效應的公司。此選股策略錨定於長期宏觀主題，包括工業回流、基礎設施現代化、人口老化以及對節能需求的日益增長。

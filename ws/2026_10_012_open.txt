== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening process prioritizes long-term fundamental quality, high returns on invested capital (ROIC), durable competitive moats, and financial discipline, intentionally ignoring short-term momentum and viral media interest. Stocks with market capitalization exceeding $50B or ranking within the top 50 of the S&P 500 were filtered out to avoid over-indexed mega-caps. Quantitative filters required positive free cash flow (or a near-term path to positive FCF), manageable debt structures (Debt/Equity generally below 1.5), forward EPS growth outpacing revenue growth (signaling operating margin expansion), and attractive valuation relative to growth (preferring PEG < 1.5). Key thematic drivers informing selection include industrial reshoring, data center thermal management, grid modernization, specialized E&S insurance, non-discretionary healthcare/bioprocessing services, and niche B2B engineering solutions.

== 選股方法論 ==
篩選過程優先考量長期基本面質量、高投入資本回報率（ROIC）、持久的競爭護城河以及財務紀律，故意忽略短期動能與病毒式媒體熱度。市值超過 500 億美元或排名在標普 500 指數前 50 名內的股票已被過濾，以避免權重過高的超大型股。定量篩選要求具備正向自由現金流（或短期內轉正的明確路徑）、可控的負債結構（負債權益比通常低於 1.5）、預估每股盈餘成長超越營收成長（意味著營運利益率擴張），以及相對於成長性具吸引力的估值（優先選擇本益成長比 PEG < 1.5）。主導選股的核心主題包括工業本土回流、資料中心熱能管理、電網現代化、專業超額與剩餘保險（E&S）、非必需性醫療/生物加工服務以及利基型 B2B 工程解決方案。

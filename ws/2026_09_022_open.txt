== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening logic utilized to build this watchlist focused on identifying high-quality, mid-cap compounders while systematically filtering out mega-caps and highly volatile, momentum-driven "media darlings." To achieve this, the universe was restricted to US-listed companies with market caps between $500 million and $50 billion. Growth and value were balanced by prioritizing companies with a forward PEG ratio under or near 1.5, positive free cash flow yields, and conservative debt-to-equity ratios. The selection was heavily informed by secular macro themes, including industrial reshoring, infrastructure modernization, automation in response to labor shortages, demographic shifts in healthcare, and the transition toward resource efficiency (smart water, composite materials, and energy-efficient power conversion). This ensures that each business is supported by structural tailwinds rather than short-term market hype.

== 選股方法論 ==
構建此觀察名單的篩選邏輯側重於識別高品質、中型市值的複利型企業，同時系統性地過濾掉超大市值公司以及高波動、動能驅動的「媒體寵兒」。為實現這一目標，選股範圍被限制在市值介於 5 億美元至 500 億美元之間的美國上市公司。通過優先考慮預估本益成長比（PEG）低於或接近 1.5、自由現金流殖利率為正且負債權益比保守的公司，來平衡增長與價值。選股決策深受長期宏觀主題的啟發，包括工業回流、基礎設施現代化、應對勞動力短缺的自動化、醫療保健領域的人口結構轉變，以及向資源效率（智慧水務、複合材料和高效電源轉換）的過渡。這確保了每家企業都受到結構性順風的支持，而非短期市場炒作。

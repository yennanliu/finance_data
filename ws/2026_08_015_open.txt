== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening logic used to construct this watchlist focused on identifying high-quality, mid-cap companies ($500M–$50B) with durable competitive advantages and strong cash-flow generation, while systematically filtering out overhyped, high-multiple market darlings. We prioritized companies with a PEG ratio below 1.5, manageable debt, and positive free cash flow, ensuring a strong margin of safety. Growth and value were balanced by targeting businesses with double-digit EPS trajectories that trade at reasonable valuations due to being misunderstood or overlooked by mainstream media. Key macro themes informing these selections include the ongoing US infrastructure revitalization, the secular transition to advanced semiconductor packaging, demographic tailwinds in healthcare, and the rising demand for energy-efficient building systems.

== 選股方法論 ==
構建此觀察名單的篩選邏輯側重於識別具有持久競爭優勢和強勁現金流產生能力的高品質中型企業（市值介於 5 億至 500 億美元之間），同時系統性地過濾掉被過度炒作、高估值的市場寵兒。我們優先考慮本益成長比（PEG）低於 1.5、負債可控且自由現金流為正的公司，以確保強大的安全邊際。我們透過鎖定因被主流媒體誤解或忽視而交易於合理估值、且每股盈餘（EPS）呈雙位數成長軌跡的企業，來平衡成長與價值。指導這些選擇的核心總體經濟主題包括持續進行的美國基礎設施振興、向先進半導體封裝的長期轉型、醫療保健領域的人口老化利多，以及對節能建築系統日益增長的需求。

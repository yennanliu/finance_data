== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==

The screening framework used for this watchlist deliberately eliminates all mega-cap technology giants, top-50 S&P 500 components, and highly volatile momentum/meme stocks to focus exclusively on under-followed, fundamentally sound small-to-mid cap businesses ($500M–$50B market capitalization). The quantitative screening filtered for positive top-line growth, double-digit forward EPS trajectories, manageable debt levels (Debt/Equity < 1.0 in most cases), and attractive valuation structures prioritized by a PEG ratio under 1.5–2.0 combined with solid Free Cash Flow (FCF) yields. Qualitative filters required explicit competitive moats—such as proprietary technical standards, dominant regional distribution networks, single-source regulatory approvals, or specialized niche engineering expertise. Macroeconomic themes informing these selections include long-term power grid modernization, U.S. infrastructure funding rollout, industrial reshoring, water efficiency mandates, and specialized healthcare delivery platforms.

== 選股方法論 ==

本觀察名單所採用的篩選框架刻意排除所有巨型科技巨頭、標普 500 指數前 50 大成分股以及高波動的動量/迷因股，從而專注於未被市場充分關注、基本面紮實的中小型企業（市值介於 5 億美元至 500 億美元之間）。定量篩選標準重點關注正向的頂線營收成長、雙位數的預估每股盈餘成長軌跡、可控的負債水平（大多數情況下負債權益比 < 1.0），以及以本益成長比（PEG）低於 1.5–2.0 結合穩健自由現金流殖利率為優先的具吸引力估值結構。定性篩選則要求企業具備明確的競爭護城河——例如專有技術標準、區域性主導分銷網絡、單一來源的監管批准或專業的小眾工程技術知識。引導這些選股主題的宏觀經濟背景包括長期電網現代化、美國基礎設施資金落地、工業製造業回流、水資源利用效率法規限制以及專業醫療保健遞送平台。

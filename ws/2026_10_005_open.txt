== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening process designed for this watchlist systematically filters out highly speculative, overvalued, and momentum-driven equities to isolate fundamentally strong, under-the-radar compounders. First, we excluded all mega-cap companies and any stocks currently experiencing high retail trading volume or intense media coverage to avoid "hype premiums." We then applied strict quantitative filters: positive and growing free cash flow, manageable debt-to-equity ratios, and consistent historical and projected revenue and EPS growth. We prioritized companies with a forward PEG ratio below 1.5, ensuring that we are not overpaying for growth. Finally, we qualitatively assessed each company's competitive moat—such as high switching costs, regulatory integration, or dominant niche market share—and aligned our selections with structural macroeconomic tailwinds, including infrastructure spending, manufacturing reshoring, and resource conservation.

== 選股方法論 ==
本自選股清單的篩選流程旨在系統性地過濾掉高投機性、估值過高以及動能驅動的股票，從而找出基本面強勁且未受市場過度關注的複利成長股。首先，我們排除了所有巨型股以及目前交易量極高或媒體高度關注的股票，以避免「炒作溢價」。接著，我們應用了嚴格的定量篩選：正向且持續增長的自由現金流、可控的負債權益比，以及穩定且具備預期成長性的營收與每股盈餘。我們優先考慮預估本益成長比（PEG）低於 1.5 的公司，以確保我們沒有為增長支付過高的代價。最後，我們定性評估了每家公司的競爭護城河（如高轉換成本、法規整合或主導性的利基市場份額），並使我們的選擇與結構性宏觀經濟利好因素（包括基礎設施支出、製造業回流和資源保護）保持一致。

== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
Our quantitative and qualitative screening process strictly eliminated all S&P 500 top-50 mega-caps, meme/momentum stocks, and heavily hyped media stocks to focus exclusively on under-the-radar small-to-mid-cap compounders ($500M–$50B market cap). We prioritized fundamental quality: balance sheet strength (low leverage, net cash positions), durable competitive moats (high switching costs, network effects, regional monopolies), and profitable growth trajectories. Valuations were screened to ensure favorable growth alignment, prioritizing companies with PEG ratios ideally below 1.5. Sector selection was informed by powerful secular themes, including power grid modernization, nearshoring/automation, specialized healthcare demand, infrastructure renewals, and mission-critical cloud software.

== 選股方法論 ==
我們的定量與定性篩選流程嚴格過濾掉了所有標普500前50大巨頭、高波動的迷因股（Meme stocks）以及被媒體過度炒作的熱門股票，專注於市值介於5億至500億美元之間的低調中小型「複利成長股」。我們優先考量基本面品質：資產負債表強度（低槓桿、淨現金狀態）、持久的經濟護城河（高轉換成本、網路效應、區域壟斷）以及可持續獲利的成長軌跡。估值經過嚴格審視以確保成長與價格相匹配，優先選擇本益成長比（PEG）原則上低於1.5的公司。產業選擇受到強大的結構性長線趨勢驅動，包含電網升級 modernization、近岸製造/自動化、專業醫療保健需求、基礎建設更新以及關鍵雲端軟體。

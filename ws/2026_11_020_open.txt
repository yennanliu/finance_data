== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening process deliberately excluded the S&P 500 top-50 mega-caps, meme/momentum stocks, and companies dominating mainstream media hype. Quantitative filters prioritized market caps between $500M and $50B, low forward PEG ratios (< 1.5 preferred), clean balance sheets with manageable debt (Debt/Equity < 0.8), and robust free cash flow yields. Qualitative screening focused on structural competitive moats—such as proprietary software integration, high customer switching costs, regulatory barriers, and localized market dominance. The portfolio reflects multi-year structural themes: US industrial reshoring, grid infrastructure modernization, automated labor-saving tools, defensive healthcare services, and disciplined specialty insurance underwriters.

== 選股方法論 ==
篩選過程特意排除了標普 500 指數前 50 大巨型市值股、迷因/動能股以及佔據主流媒體炒作焦點的公司。定量篩選優先考慮市值介於 5 億至 500 億美元之間的企業、較低的預估本益成長比（PEG < 1.5 為佳）、負債可控的健全資產負債表（負債權益比 < 0.8）以及強勁的自由現金流殖利率。定性篩選則著重於結構性競爭護城河——例如獨家軟體整合、高客戶轉換成本、監管壁壘及區域市場主導地位。該投資組合反映了多年期的結構性主題：美國工業回流、電網基礎設施現代化、自動化節省人力工具、防禦性醫療保健服務以及具備紀律的專業保險承保商。

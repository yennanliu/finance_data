== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening logic utilized to build this watchlist focused on filtering out high-beta, momentum-driven, and mega-cap stocks to isolate high-quality compounders trading at attractive valuations relative to their growth prospects. We prioritized companies with market caps between $500M and $50B, requiring positive free cash flow, manageable debt (typically Debt/Equity < 0.5), and a forward PEG ratio ideally below 1.5. Rather than chasing speculative tech or viral consumer brands, we weighted consistent earnings power, high returns on invested capital (ROIC), and clear competitive moats—such as high switching costs, regulatory barriers, or proprietary technology. The selection is heavily informed by structural, multi-decade macro themes, including US manufacturing nearshoring, electrical grid modernization, water scarcity/infrastructure upgrades, and the automation of industrial and food services.

== 選股方法論 ==
用於建立此觀察名單的篩選邏輯側重於過濾掉高貝塔值、動能驅動和超大型股，以分離出相對於其成長前景而言估值具有吸引力的高品質複合成長股。我們優先考慮市值在5億至500億美元之間的公司，要求其具有正的自由現金流、可控的債務（通常負債權益比 < 0.5），且預估本益成長比（PEG）最好低於1.5。我們沒有追逐投機性科技股或病毒式傳播的消費品牌，而是權衡了持續的獲利能力、高投資資本回報率（ROIC）以及明確的競爭護城河（如高轉換成本、監管壁壘或專有技術）。此選股在很大程度上受到結構性、跨越數十年的宏觀主題的啟發，包括美國製造業回流、電網現代化、水資源短缺/基礎設施升級，以及工業和餐飲服務的自動化。

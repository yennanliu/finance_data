== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening process was designed to filter out market noise, speculative momentum, and overvalued mega-cap equities, focusing instead on high-quality compounders. We excluded companies with market caps exceeding $50 billion and those with excessive media coverage or speculative valuations. Stocks were ranked based on a balanced weighting of structural growth drivers (such as reshoring, infrastructure spending, and demographic shifts), high return on invested capital (ROIC), and reasonable valuations (targeting a PEG ratio below 1.5). Balance sheet health was prioritized by selecting firms with low debt-to-equity ratios and robust free cash flow yields, ensuring resilience across various macroeconomic environments.

== 選股方法論 ==
篩選過程旨在過濾市場噪音、投機性動能和估值過高的超大型股，轉而專注於高品質的複合成長股。我們排除了市值超過 500 億美元以及媒體過度報導或估值具投機性的公司。個股排名基於結構性成長驅動因素（如製造業回流、基礎建設支出和人口結構轉變）、高投入資本回報率（ROIC）以及合理估值（目標本益成長比 PEG 低於 1.5）的平衡權重。我們優先考慮資產負債表的健康狀況，選擇負債權益比較低且自由現金流殖利率強勁的企業，以確保在各種總體經濟環境中的韌性。

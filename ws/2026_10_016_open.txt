== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==

The screening framework enforced strict exclusion criteria: mega-cap S&P top-50 constituents, heavily covered media darlings, momentum-driven AI high-flyers, and unprofitable speculative stocks were systematically eliminated. Portfolio candidates were required to fall within the $500M to $50B market cap range while demonstrating solid balance sheet discipline (manageable debt-to-equity ratios and reliable positive free cash flow). Stock selection balanced earnings growth quality against valuation by prioritizing companies with PEG ratios under 1.5, forward EPS growth exceeding 10%, and defensible moats—such as regulatory compliance requirements, proprietary technological specifications, high switching costs, or local distribution density. The thematic composition emphasizes secular tailwinds in electric grid infrastructure, industrial nearshoring, healthcare outsourcing, and niche building materials conversion.

== 選股方法論 ==

本篩選框架執行嚴格的排除標準：S&P 500 前 50 大超大型市值股、媒體熱捧股、動量驅動的 AI 炒作股以及未獲利的投機性股票均被系統性排除。入選公司市值須介於 5 億至 500 億美元之間，並展現出穩健的資產負債表紀律（可控的負債權益比與可靠的正自由現金流）。選股過程平衡了盈餘品質與估值，優先考慮 PEG 低於 1.5、預估每股盈餘成長率高於 10% 且擁有可防禦護城河的公司——例如法規合規要求、專有技術規範、高切換成本或在地分銷密度。產業佈局強調電網基礎設施、工業近岸外包、醫療外包及特殊建材轉換等長期結構性順風。

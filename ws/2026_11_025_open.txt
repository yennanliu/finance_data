== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening framework filtered out mega-cap equities (S&P 500 top-50), high-beta momentum tickers, and financial media consensus darlings. The target universe focused exclusively on small-to-mid cap US equities ($500M to $50B) possessing disciplined capital allocation, conservative balance sheets (low net debt or net cash balance), and predictable cash flows. Quantitative metrics prioritized strong forward EPS acceleration, top-line growth consistency, and attractive valuation (targeting PEG < 1.5 and high FCF yield). Qualitatively, companies were selected based on durable competitive moats—such as high switching costs, proprietary IP, regulatory barriers, or regional density—benefiting from long-term secular tailwinds including data center power/cooling demand, domestic industrial reshoring, aerospace fleet maintenance, and healthcare infrastructure spending.

== 選股方法論 ==
本選股架構嚴格過濾掉巨型市值股（S&P 500 前 50 大）、高貝塔係數的動能股以及主流財經媒體熱炒標的。目標範圍專注於市值介於 5 億至 500 億美元之間、具備嚴謹資本配置、保守資產負債表（低淨負債或淨現金）及可預測現金流的美國中小盤股。定量指標優先考慮強勁的預估每股盈餘（EPS）成長加速、營收成長穩定度以及具吸引力的估值（目標 PEG < 1.5 且自由現金流殖利率高）。定性方面，選股基於持久的競爭護城河——例如高客戶轉換成本、專利技術 IP、監管壁壘或區域密度優勢——同時受益於長期結構性順風，包括資料中心電力/散熱需求、美國本土工業製造業回流、航空航天車隊維護以及醫療保健基礎設施支出。

== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening logic focused on identifying high-quality, mid-cap compounders ($500M–$50B) with strong balance sheets and sustainable competitive advantages, while systematically filtering out overhyped, high-multiple momentum stocks. We prioritized companies with a PEG ratio near or below 1.5, positive free cash flow, and low debt-to-equity ratios. To filter out "hot" stocks, we excluded companies with extreme valuation multiples (e.g., P/S > 15x) or those frequently featured in retail trading forums. Our sector allocation was heavily informed by structural, multi-decade macro themes, including the modernization of municipal water infrastructure, the reshoring of US manufacturing, advanced semiconductor packaging, and the secular demand for energy efficiency and specialty healthcare services.

== 選股方法論 ==
選股邏輯專注於識別具有強大資產負債表和可持續競爭優勢的高品質、中型股複利企業（市值介於 5 億至 500 億美元之間），同時系統性地過濾掉過度炒作、高估值的動能股。我們優先考慮本益成長比（PEG）接近或低於 1.5、自由現金流為正且負債權益比較低的企業。為了過濾掉「熱門」股票，我們排除了具有極端估值倍數（例如市銷率 P/S > 15x）或在零售交易論壇中頻繁出現的公司。我們的行業配置深受結構性、跨越數十年的宏觀主題影響，包括市政水務基礎設施的現代化、美國製造業的回流、先進半導體封裝，以及對節能和特種醫療保健服務的長期需求。

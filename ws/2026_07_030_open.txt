== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
Our selection methodology focused on identifying high-quality, mid-to-small-cap companies ($500M–$50B) that operate outside the mainstream financial media spotlight. We filtered out highly volatile momentum stocks, mega-cap tech giants, and companies trading at speculative valuations. Instead, we prioritized businesses with durable competitive moats (such as regulatory barriers, proprietary technology, or high switching costs), strong balance sheets (low debt-to-equity and robust free cash flow), and reasonable valuations relative to their growth prospects (targeting a PEG ratio near or below 1.5). Key structural themes informing our picks include industrial automation, infrastructure modernization, water conservation, and demographic-driven services.

== 選股方法論 ==
我們的選股方法論專注於尋找在主流財經媒體聚光燈之外營運的高品質、中小型市值公司（5億至500億美元）。我們過濾掉了高波動性的動能股、巨型科技龍頭以及估值具投機性的公司。相反，我們優先考慮具有持久競爭護城河（如監管壁壘、專有技術或高轉換成本）、強健資產負債表（低負債權益比和強勁的自由現金流）以及相對於其成長前景估值合理（目標本益成長比 PEG 接近或低於 1.5）的企業。啟發我們選擇的核心結構性主題包括工業自動化、基礎設施現代化、水資源保護以及人口結構驅動的服務。

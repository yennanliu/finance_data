== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening framework prioritizes high-quality, durable compounders operating within the $500M to $50B market cap spectrum while excluding the S&P 500 top-50 mega-caps, high-beta momentum plays, and media darlings. Quantitative filters targeted stocks delivering double-digit forward EPS growth, conservative balance sheets (Debt/Equity typically under 0.8), strong free cash flow conversion, and favorable valuation profiles anchored by a PEG ratio under 1.5. Qualitatively, the methodology seeks out companies possessing structural competitive moats—such as high switching costs, proprietary domain technologies, distribution network scale, or regulatory tailwinds—and operating within secular growth vectors including industrial automation, electrical grid modernization, specialized healthcare, and infrastructure reshoring.

== 選股方法論 ==
本選股篩選架構優先考慮在 5 億至 500 億美元市值範圍內營運的高品質、持久複利型公司，同時排除標普 500 前 50 大巨頭、高 Beta 值的動能炒作股及媒體寵兒。定量篩選目標為提供雙位數預估每股盈餘成長、保守資產負債表（負債權益比通常低於 0.8）、強勁的自由現金流轉化率，以及本益成長比（PEG）低於 1.5 所支撐的優良估值 profile 的股票。在定性方面，本方法論尋找擁有結構性競爭護城河（如高轉換成本、專利領域技術、分銷網絡規模或法規順風）的公司，且這些公司營運於工業自動化、電網現代化、專門醫療保健及基礎設施回流等長期成長趨勢中。

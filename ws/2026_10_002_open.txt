== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening process focused on identifying high-quality, resilient businesses trading at reasonable valuations while deliberately excluding mega-cap tech and highly speculative momentum stocks. We filtered out companies with market caps exceeding $50 billion and those with excessive media coverage or unsustainable valuations (PEG > 1.5 was generally avoided, with exceptions only for exceptionally high-moat businesses). Weight was heavily placed on free cash flow generation, low debt-to-equity ratios, and structural tailwinds—such as the domestic manufacturing renaissance, water infrastructure modernization, and specialized healthcare niches. This balanced approach ensures the watchlist comprises durable compounders capable of navigating macroeconomic uncertainty.

== 選股方法論 ==
篩選過程專注於尋找估值合理、具備韌性的優質企業，同時刻意排除超大市值科技股和高度投機的動能股。我們過濾掉了市值超過500億美元的公司，以及媒體過度報導或估值不可持續的公司（通常避免本益成長比 PEG > 1.5 的股票，僅對具有極高護城河的企業破例）。我們高度重視自由現金流生成能力、低負債權益比以及結構性順風——例如國內製造業復興、水資源基礎設施現代化以及專業醫療保健利基市場。這種平衡的方法確保了觀察名單由能夠應對總體經濟不確定性的耐用複利型企業組成。

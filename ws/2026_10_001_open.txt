== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening logic used to generate this watchlist focused on identifying high-quality, under-the-radar compounders while systematically filtering out mega-caps and speculative market favorites. To eliminate "hot" stocks, we excluded any company in the S&P 500 top-50 by market cap, as well as companies with extreme social media sentiment or valuations detached from fundamentals (e.g., P/E > 40x without exceptional growth). We prioritized a balanced mix of growth and value, weighting a low PEG ratio (< 1.5) and robust free cash flow (FCF) yield heavily. Balance sheet health was non-negotiable, requiring low debt-to-equity ratios or clear paths to rapid deleveraging. The selection was informed by strong secular macro themes: domestic manufacturing reshoring, aging demographics, water and environmental infrastructure upgrades, and the increasing demand for automation and energy efficiency.

== 選股方法論 ==
用於生成此觀察名單的篩選邏輯側重於識別高質量、被低估的複利成長股，同時系統性地過濾掉超大市值股和投機性的市場熱門股。為了消除「熱門」股票，我們排除了市值排名前 50 的標普 500 指數成分股，以及社交媒體情緒極端或估值脫離基本面（例如在沒有異常成長的情況下本益比 > 40x）的公司。我們優先考慮成長與價值的平衡，重點權衡低本益成長比（PEG < 1.5）和強勁的自由現金流（FCF）殖利率。資產負債表的健康狀況是不可妥協的，要求低負債權益比或有明確的快速去槓桿化路徑。選股受到強大的結構性宏觀主題啟發：國內製造業回流、人口老化、水資源和環境基礎設施升級，以及對自動化和能源效率日益增長的需求。

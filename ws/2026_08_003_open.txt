== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
Our screening process was designed to identify high-quality, under-the-radar compounders by filtering out the top 50 S&P 500 mega-caps and eliminating high-beta momentum stocks or media-hyped names. We prioritized companies with market caps between $500M and $50B that exhibit strong structural tailwinds—such as US manufacturing reshoring, infrastructure modernization, and demographic shifts. We balanced growth and value by targeting companies with consistent revenue and EPS growth, expanding operating margins, and a PEG ratio below 1.5. Finally, we required robust balance sheets (low debt-to-equity) and high free cash flow yields to ensure these businesses can self-fund their growth and withstand macroeconomic volatility.

== 選股方法論 ==
我們的篩選流程旨在透過過濾掉標普 500 指數前 50 大超大型股，並排除高貝塔值（high-beta）的動能股或媒體炒作的股票，來識別高質量、被低估的複利型企業。我們優先考慮市值在 5 億至 500 億美元之間、且展現出強大結構性順風（如美國製造業回流、基礎設施現代化和人口結構變化）的公司。我們透過鎖定營收和每股盈餘（EPS）持續成長、營業利益率擴大且本益成長比（PEG）低於 1.5 的公司，來平衡成長與價值。最後，我們要求具備強健的資產負債表（低負債權益比）和高自由現金流殖利率，以確保這些企業能夠自主資助其成長並抵禦總體經濟的波動。

== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
Our screening process prioritizes "quality compounders" and "hidden champions"—companies with dominant market shares in highly specialized niches, robust pricing power, and structural tailwinds that insulate them from macroeconomic volatility. We systematically filtered out the top 50 S&P 500 mega-caps and high-beta momentum stocks by setting a market cap ceiling of $50B and excluding companies with extreme retail trading volumes or excessive media coverage. Instead, we weighted capital efficiency (ROIC), consistent free cash flow generation, and reasonable valuations (targeting PEG ratios below 1.5). The selection is heavily informed by structural macro themes: the modernization of the US electrical grid, municipal water infrastructure upgrades, industrial automation to combat labor shortages, and defensive healthcare niches.

== 選股方法論 ==
我們的篩選流程優先考慮「優質複利股」和「隱形冠軍」——即在高度專業化的利基市場中擁有主導市場份額、強大定價權以及能抵禦總體經濟波動的結構性利多的公司。我們系統性地過濾掉了標普 500 指數前 50 大巨型股和高貝塔值（high-beta）的動能股，方法是將市值上限設定為 500 億美元，並排除散戶交易量極端或媒體過度報導的公司。相反，我們加大了對資本效率（ROIC）、持續的自由現金流產生能力以及合理估值（目標本益成長比 PEG 低於 1.5）的權重。此選股名單深受結構性總體主題的啟發：美國電網現代化、市政水務基礎設施升級、應對勞動力短缺的工業自動化，以及防禦性的醫療保健利基市場。

== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening process focused on identifying high-quality, mid-to-small-cap compounders ($500M to $50B) while systematically filtering out mega-caps, high-momentum "meme" stocks, and overhyped media darlings. We excluded companies with forward P/E ratios exceeding 35x or those with extreme retail trading volumes. Instead, we prioritized businesses with a PEG ratio below 1.5, positive free cash flow yields, and clean balance sheets (low or zero net debt). Weight was heavily given to companies possessing structural competitive moats—such as high customer switching costs, proprietary technology, or dominant market shares in niche industries. The selection was informed by long-term macroeconomic themes, including US manufacturing reshoring, infrastructure modernization, water conservation, and secular demographic shifts (aging population).

== 選股方法論 ==
篩選過程專注於尋找高質量的中小型複利成長股（市值 5 億至 500 億美元），同時系統性地過濾掉超大市值股、高動能的「迷因股」以及被媒體過度炒作的熱門股。我們排除了預估本益比超過 35 倍或散戶交易量極端的公司。相反地，我們優先考慮本益成長比（PEG）低於 1.5、自由現金流殖利率為正且資產負債表乾淨（淨負債極低或為零）的企業。我們高度看重具備結構性競爭護城河的公司，例如高客戶轉換成本、專有技術或在利基行業中佔據主導地位的市佔率。此選股名單結合了長期宏觀經濟主題，包括美國製造業回流、基礎設施現代化、水資源保護以及長期的老齡化人口結構轉變。

== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening process was designed to filter out market noise, high-beta momentum plays, and overvalued mega-caps, focusing instead on high-quality compounders. We restricted the universe to US-listed equities with market caps between $500M and $50B, excluding S&P 500 top-50 names and stocks with extreme retail trading volumes (meme stocks). We prioritized companies with a PEG ratio under 1.5, positive free cash flow yields, and strong balance sheets (low debt-to-equity). The selection was heavily weighted toward secular macro themes: the US manufacturing renaissance (reshoring), critical infrastructure upgrades (water, power, data centers), demographic shifts (aging population requiring specialized healthcare), and niche technological moats (specialty alloys, advanced optical packaging, and proprietary SaaS networks). This balance ensures a portfolio of resilient, cash-generative businesses positioned for long-term outperformance.

== 選股方法論 ==
篩選過程旨在過濾市場噪音、高貝塔值的動能炒作股以及估值過高的巨型股，轉而專注於高品質的複利成長股。我們將選股範圍限制在市值介於 5 億至 500 億美元之間的美國上市公司，排除了標普 500 指數前 50 大公司以及散戶交易量極端的股票（迷因股）。我們優先考慮本益成長比（PEG）低於 1.5、自由現金流殖利率為正且資產負債表強健（低負債權益比）的公司。選股權重高度傾向於長期的宏觀主題：美國製造業復興（回流）、關鍵基礎設施升級（水務、電力、數據中心）、人口結構變化（需要專業醫療保健的老齡化人口）以及利基技術護城河（特種合金、先進光學封裝和專有 SaaS 網路）。這種平衡確保了投資組合由具備韌性、能產生現金且定位於長期優於大盤表現的企業組成。

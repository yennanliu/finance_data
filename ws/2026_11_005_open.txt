== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening process deliberately excludes mega-cap technology darlings, high-profile media favorites, and speculative momentum stocks. Instead, it filters for US-listed companies in the small-to-mid-cap sweet spot ($500M to $50B market cap) exhibiting high fundamental quality. Emphasis was placed on structural growth tailwinds—such as power grid modernization, AI data center thermal management, reshoring of manufacturing, aerospace recovery cycles, and demographic shifts in healthcare—where structural demand remains durable across market cycles. Candidates were weighted based on balance sheet health (low debt-to-equity, robust free cash flow), identifiable competitive moats (pricing power, high customer switching costs, regulatory barriers), and attractive growth-to-valuation ratios (preferring a PEG ratio under 1.5). The result is a balanced portfolio of under-the-radar market leaders capable of delivering superior long-term risk-adjusted returns.

== 選股方法論 ==
本選股篩選流程刻意排除了巨型科技龍頭、媒體熱門焦點股以及投機性動量股。相反地，篩選機制聚焦於市值介於 5 億至 500 億美元之間、具備高基本面品質的美國中小型上市公司。選股核心在於捕捉跨越經濟週期仍具備耐久需求的結構性成長趨勢——例如電網現代化、AI 數據中心熱管理、製造業回流、航太復甦週期以及醫療保健人口老齡化趨勢。所有標的分別根據資產負債表健康度（低負債權益比、強勁自由現金流）、明確的競爭護城河（定價能力、高客戶轉移成本、監管壁壘）以及具吸引力的成長估值比（優先選擇 PEG 低於 1.5）進行權重排序。最終呈現出這組兼具高品質、未被市場過度關注且具備優異長期風險調整後回報潛力的市場領航者觀察清單。

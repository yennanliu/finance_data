== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
Our screening process systematically filters out the top 50 S&P 500 companies and high-multiple momentum stocks to focus exclusively on small-to-mid-cap compounders ($500M–$50B) that operate away from the media spotlight. We heavily weight structural growth drivers (such as infrastructure modernization, water scarcity, industrial automation, and specialized B2B software) against valuation, prioritizing companies with a PEG ratio below 1.5, high return on invested capital (ROIC), and robust free cash flow. By focusing on businesses with high switching costs, proprietary technology, or dominant niche market shares, this methodology identifies resilient compounders capable of delivering long-term outperformance across varying macroeconomic cycles.

== 選股方法論 ==
我們的篩選流程系統性地過濾掉標普 500 指數前 50 大公司和高估值的動能股，專注於遠離媒體聚光燈的中小型複利股（市值在 5 億至 500 億美元之間）。我們在評估估值時，高度權衡結構性成長驅動因素（如基礎設施現代化、水資源短缺、工業自動化和專業 B2B 軟體），優先考慮本益成長比（PEG）低於 1.5、高投資報酬率（ROIC）和強勁自由現金流的公司。通過專注於具有高轉換成本、專有技術或主導性利基市場份額的企業，該方法論識別出能夠在不同宏觀經濟週期中實現長期超額回報的韌性複利股。

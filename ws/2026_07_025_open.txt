== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening process was designed to filter out highly speculative, over-hyped, and overvalued mega-cap stocks, focusing instead on high-quality mid-and-small-cap compounders. We eliminated any stock with a market cap exceeding $50 billion or those frequently trending on social media and mainstream retail trading platforms. We prioritized companies with a PEG ratio below 1.5, positive free cash flow, and low debt-to-equity ratios. The selection was heavily informed by secular macroeconomic themes, including the modernization of the US electrical and water infrastructure, industrial reshoring, advanced semiconductor packaging, and demographic shifts in healthcare. This methodology ensures a portfolio of resilient businesses with strong competitive moats and clear growth trajectories that are overlooked by the broader market.

== 選股方法論 ==
篩選過程旨在過濾掉投機性強、過度炒作且估值過高的超大型股，轉而專注於優質的中小型股複利企業。我們排除了任何市值超過 500 億美元或在社交媒體和主流散戶交易平台上頻繁走紅的股票。我們優先考慮本益成長比（PEG）低於 1.5、自由現金流為正且負債權益比低的公司。選股深受長期宏觀經濟主題的啟發，包括美國電網和水基礎設施的現代化、工業回流、先進半導體封裝以及醫療保健領域的人口結構轉變。這種方法論確保了投資組合由具備強大競爭護城河和清晰成長軌跡、且被大盤所忽視的韌性企業組成。

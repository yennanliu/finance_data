== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening process was designed to filter out mega-cap tech stocks, speculative meme stocks, and overhyped market darlings, focusing instead on high-quality mid- and small-cap companies ($500M to $50B) with durable competitive advantages. We prioritized companies with strong balance sheets (low debt-to-equity), consistent free cash flow generation, and reasonable valuations relative to their growth prospects (targeting a PEG ratio under 1.5). The selection is heavily informed by structural macroeconomic themes, including industrial reshoring, infrastructure modernization, climate resilience, and the outsourcing of specialized services. By focusing on B2B niches, mission-critical components, and highly regulated industries, this methodology identifies resilient compounders capable of delivering superior risk-adjusted returns over a multi-year horizon.

== 選股方法論 ==
篩選過程旨在過濾掉超大型科技股、投機性迷因股和過度炒作的市場寵兒，轉而關注具有持久競爭優勢的高質量中小型公司（市值在 5 億至 500 億美元之間）。我們優先考慮資產負債表強勁（低負債權益比）、持續產生自由現金流且估值相對於其成長前景合理（目標本益成長比 PEG 低於 1.5）的公司。選股深受結構性宏觀經濟主題的啟發，包括工業回流、基礎設施現代化、氣候韌性以及專業服務的外包。通過專注於 B2B 利基市場、關鍵任務組件和高度受監管的行業，該方法論識別出了能夠在多年週期內提供優異風險調整後回報的具韌性複利型企業。

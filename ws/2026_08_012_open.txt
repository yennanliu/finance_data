== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening process was designed to filter out high-multiple, media-hyped momentum stocks in favor of high-quality, under-the-radar compounders. We restricted our universe to US-listed companies with market caps between $500 million and $50 billion, deliberately avoiding the S&P 500 top-50 to eliminate mega-cap bias. Stocks were filtered to ensure positive free cash flow (or a clear near-term path to it), manageable debt-to-equity ratios, and a forward PEG ratio ideally below 1.5. We prioritized companies with strong structural tailwinds—such as domestic manufacturing reshoring, infrastructure modernization, water scarcity, and specialized healthcare niches—where the business possesses a clear competitive moat (e.g., regulatory barriers, high switching costs, or proprietary technology) that protects its long-term compounding potential.

== 選股方法論 ==
篩選過程旨在過濾掉高估值、媒體過度炒作的動能股，轉而選擇高質量、不引人注目的長期複利型企業。我們將選股範圍限制在市值介於 5 億美元至 500 億美元之間的美國上市公司，刻意避開標普 500 指數前 50 大企業，以消除超大型股的偏差。我們對股票進行了篩選，以確保其擁有正向的自由現金流（或明確的近期實現路徑）、可控的負債權益比，以及理想情況下低於 1.5 的預估本益成長比 (PEG)。我們優先考慮具有強大結構性利好的公司——例如本土製造業回流、基礎設施現代化、水資源短缺以及專業醫療保健利基市場——在這些領域中，企業擁有明確的競爭護城河（如監管壁壘、高轉換成本或專有技術），以保護其長期的複利潛力。

== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening process prioritized companies with demonstrable long-term secular growth drivers, strong competitive moats, and solid financial health, while deliberately avoiding the S&P 500 top-50 mega-caps and any stock frequently highlighted in mainstream financial media or exhibiting meme-like volatility. I focused on sectors undergoing significant transformation or benefiting from structural tailwinds such as digital transformation, renewable energy, healthcare innovation, and specialized industrial technology. Valuation was a key filter, with a preference for PEG ratios below 1.5, though exceptions were made for companies with exceptionally strong moats, consistent high-quality earnings, and robust free cash flow generation. Balance sheet strength, indicated by manageable debt and positive free cash flow, was non-negotiable. The ranking reflects a blend of conviction in the investment thesis, perceived undervaluation, and the durability of the competitive advantage.

== 選股方法論 ==
選股過程優先考慮具有明確長期結構性成長驅動力、強大競爭護城河和穩健財務狀況的公司，同時刻意避開標普500指數前50名的巨型股以及任何經常在主流財經媒體上被報導或表現出迷因股波動性的股票。我專注於正在經歷重大轉型或受惠於結構性順風的行業，例如數位轉型、再生能源、醫療保健創新和專業工業技術。估值是一個關鍵篩選條件，偏好本益成長比(PEG)低於1.5的公司，但對於具有特別強大護城河、持續高品質收益和強勁自由現金流生成能力的公司，則會有所例外。資產負債表的穩健性，以可控的債務和正自由現金流為指標，是不可協商的條件。排名反映了對投資論點的信心、感知到的低估值以及競爭優勢的持久性。

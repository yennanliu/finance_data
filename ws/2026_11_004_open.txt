== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
This watchlist was constructed using a fundamental bottom-up screening protocol designed to isolate durable compounding businesses while stripping out media sentiment and macro momentum. We eliminated all mega-cap technology firms (S&P 500 top-50 by market cap) and high-volatility meme stocks. Screening criteria enforced: (1) market capitalization between $500 million and $50 billion; (2) forward P/E to EPS growth ratio (PEG) generally under 1.5x; (3) manageable debt-to-equity leverage (preferably net-cash or D/E < 0.85); and (4) positive Free Cash Flow yields above 3%. Sector weighting intentionally tilts toward domestic infrastructure re-shoring, electrical grid modernization, specialized healthcare platforms, and high-switching-cost industrial B2B distribution—sectors benefiting from secular macro tailwinds independent of consumer market shifts.

== 選股方法論 ==
本觀察名單採用自下而上的基本面篩選機制構建，旨在找出具備長期複利能力且財務穩健的公司，同時剔除媒體情緒與短期炒作動能。我們過濾掉所有超大型科技公司（按市值計算為標普 500 前 50 大）及高波動的迷因股。篩選標準包括：(1) 市值介於 5 億美元至 500 億美元之間；(2) 預估本益成長比 (PEG) 通常低於 1.5 倍；(3) 負債權益比可控（偏好淨現金或 D/E < 0.85）；(4) 正向自由現金流殖利率高於 3%。在產業權重上，戰略性偏向美國本土基礎設施回流、電網現代化、專利型醫療保健平台以及高轉換成本的工業 B2B 分銷——這些產業受益於獨立於消費市場波動的長期宏觀順風。

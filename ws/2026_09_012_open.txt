== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
This watchlist was constructed using a rigorous fundamental screen designed to identify high-quality, under-the-radar US equities while filtering out overhyped, high-momentum, or mega-cap stocks. The initial universe was restricted to US-listed companies with market caps between $500M and $50B, excluding S&P 500 top-50 constituents and stocks frequently trending on social media or mainstream financial news. We prioritized companies with a clear competitive moat (e.g., high switching costs, regulatory barriers, or geographic monopolies) and robust balance sheets, favoring low debt-to-equity ratios and positive free cash flow. Growth and valuation were balanced using a Growth-at-a-Reasonable-Price (GARP) framework, targeting a forward PEG ratio below 1.5. Key secular themes informing these selections include US industrial reshoring, infrastructure modernization, water conservation, aging demographics, and specialized B2B technology niches.

== 選股方法論 ==
本觀察名單是透過嚴格的基本面篩選構建而成，旨在識別高質量、未受市場廣泛關注的美國股票，同時過濾掉過度炒作、高動能或超大型市值的股票。初始篩選範圍限制在市值介於 5 億美元至 500 億美元之間的美國上市公司，排除標普 500 指數前 50 大成分股，以及在社群媒體或主流財經新聞中頻繁出現的熱門股票。我們優先考慮擁有清晰競爭護城河（例如高轉換成本、監管壁壘或地理壟斷）和穩健資產負債表的公司，偏好低負債權益比和正自由現金流。增長與估值之間採用「合理價格成長法」（GARP）框架進行平衡，目標是預估本益成長比（PEG）低於 1.5。指導這些選股的關鍵長期宏觀主題包括：美國工業回流、基礎設施現代化、水資源保護、人口老化趨勢以及專業的 B2B 科技利基市場。

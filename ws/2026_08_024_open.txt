== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
This screening process was designed to systematically filter out overhyped, media-driven sectors (such as mega-cap tech and speculative AI plays) in favor of fundamentally sound small-to-mid and mid-to-large cap companies ($500M to $50B) with strong competitive moats. We balanced growth and value by prioritizing companies with a forward PEG ratio under 1.5, solid balance sheets (low or zero net debt), and robust free cash flow yields. The selection was heavily informed by structural macroeconomic tailwinds, including domestic manufacturing reshoring, aging demographics, municipal water infrastructure upgrades, non-discretionary replacement cycles, and highly specialized, high-switching-cost niches in industrial automation and data security.

== 選股方法論 ==
本篩選流程旨在系統性地排除當前市場中過熱、由媒體炒作驅動的板塊（例如超大型科技股和純投機性的 AI 概念股），轉而專注於市值在 5 億至 500 億美元之間、具備強勁基本面的中小型及中大型企業。我們對「成長性」與「價值性」進行了平衡權重：優先選擇本益成長比（PEG）低於 1.5、資產負債表健全（低負債或無負債）且自由現金流殖利率強勁的公司。在宏觀和行業主題方面，我們重點佈局了具備長期結構性利多的領域，包括美國本土製造業與基礎設施回流、人口老化帶來的醫療保健需求、水資源短缺驅動的智慧公用事業升級、非自由裁量性的設備更換週期，以及高度專業化且具備高轉換成本的工業自動化與數據安全。

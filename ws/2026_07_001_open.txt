== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening process began by filtering out mega-cap companies (S&P 500 top-50 by market cap) and any stocks frequently highlighted as "hot" or "trending" in mainstream financial media, or those exhibiting meme-stock characteristics. This ensured a focus on under-the-radar opportunities. Subsequently, a multi-faceted fundamental analysis was applied, prioritizing companies demonstrating consistent double-digit revenue and EPS growth trajectories, coupled with expanding margins or a clear path to profitability. Balance sheet strength was paramount, requiring manageable debt levels and robust free cash flow generation or a credible path to achieving it. A critical filter was the presence of a durable competitive moat—such as high switching costs, proprietary technology, strong brands, or network effects—or significant structural tailwinds from sector trends, demographic shifts, or technological advancements. Valuation was assessed using forward P/E and PEG ratios, with a strong preference for PEG below 1.5, indicating growth at a reasonable price, though some high-quality compounders were included slightly above this threshold. Macro themes informing these picks included digital transformation (especially in industrials and specialized software), healthcare innovation driven by an aging population and pet humanization, industrial automation/reshoring, and sustainability/water management. The final ranking reflects a blend of growth potential, balance sheet quality, competitive advantage, and valuation attractiveness.

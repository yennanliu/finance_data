== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening logic used to construct this watchlist filters out mega-cap stocks (market cap > $50B) and highly volatile, momentum-driven media favorites. Instead, it prioritizes companies with market caps between $500M and $50B that exhibit robust fundamental health: positive free cash flow, manageable debt (Debt/Equity < 1.2), and attractive valuations relative to their growth trajectories (PEG < 1.5). Growth and value are balanced by targeting companies with double-digit forward EPS growth but reasonable forward P/E multiples. The selection is heavily informed by structural, multi-year macro themes, including US manufacturing reshoring, grid modernization, water infrastructure upgrades, defense IT security, and demographic shifts in healthcare.

== 選股方法論 ==
用於構建此觀察名單的篩選邏輯過濾掉了超大型股（市值大於 500 億美元）以及高波動性、動能驅動的媒體寵兒。相反，它優先考慮市值在 5 億美元至 500 億美元之間、且展現出強勁基本面健康狀況的公司：正自由現金流、可控債務（負債權益比小於 1.2）以及相對於其成長軌跡具有吸引力的估值（本益成長比小於 1.5）。通過鎖定具有雙位數預估每股盈餘成長但本益比合理的公司，實現了成長與價值的平衡。選股在很大程度上受到結構性、跨年度宏觀主題的啟發，包括美國製造業回流、電網現代化、水資源基礎設施升級、國防 IT 安全以及醫療保健領域的人口結構變化。

== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening logic for this watchlist was designed to filter out the top 50 S&P 500 mega-caps and high-beta momentum stocks that dominate mainstream media headlines. Instead, the focus was placed on high-quality compounders in the small-to-mid-cap sweet spot ($500M to $50B) that exhibit structural competitive advantages. Growth and value were balanced by prioritizing companies with a forward PEG ratio under or near 1.5, backed by strong balance sheets (low debt/equity) and robust free cash flow yields. The selection was heavily informed by secular macro themes, including domestic manufacturing reshoring, water scarcity and environmental regulation, infrastructure modernization, and specialized healthcare outsourcing.

== 選股方法論 ==
本觀察名單的篩選邏輯旨在過濾掉主導主流媒體頭條的前 50 大 S&P 500 超大型股和高波動的動能股。相反地，重點放在中小市值黃金區間（5 億至 500 億美元）中展現結構性競爭優勢的高質量複利型企業。在增長與價值之間取得平衡，優先選擇預估本益成長比（PEG）低於或接近 1.5 的公司，並輔以強健的資產負債表（低負債權益比）和充沛的自由現金流殖利率。選股深受長期宏觀主題的啟發，包括國內製造業回流、水資源短缺與環境法規、基礎設施現代化以及專業醫療外包。

== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
Our screening process deliberately filters out mega-cap stocks and highly publicized media darlings to focus on high-quality, under-the-radar compounders. We prioritize companies with market caps between $500 million and $50 billion that possess strong balance sheets, manageable debt, and consistent free cash flow generation. The selection heavily weights structural tailwinds—such as manufacturing reshoring, water infrastructure modernization, automation, and regulatory-driven demand—combined with robust competitive moats like high switching costs, proprietary technology, or dominant niche market shares. By focusing on reasonable valuations relative to growth (targeting PEG ratios near or below historical averages), this methodology identifies resilient businesses capable of delivering long-term, compounding returns for patient investors.

== 選股方法論 ==
我們的篩選過程刻意過濾掉超大市值股票和高度曝光的媒體寵兒，以專注於高質量、未受關注的複利型企業。我們優先考慮市值在 5 億至 500 億美元之間、資產負債表強健、負債可控且能持續產生自由現金流的公司。選股過程高度重視結構性順風因素——例如製造業回流、水務基礎設施現代化、自動化以及法規驅動的需求——並結合強大的競爭護城河，如高轉換成本、專有技術或佔主導地位的利基市場份額。通過專注於相對於成長性而言合理的估值（目標是本益成長比接近或低於歷史平均水平），本方法論旨在識別出能夠為耐心投資者提供長期複利回報的具韌性企業。

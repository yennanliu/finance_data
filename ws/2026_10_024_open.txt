== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
This watchlist was constructed by systematically screening the US equity market to identify fundamentally superior small-to-mid cap compounders ($500M–$50B market cap) while eliminating all mega-cap technology leaders, speculative momentum names, and viral retail stocks. The quantitative filter mandated positive free cash flow, manageable debt profiles (Debt/Equity < 1.0 in most sectors), forward PEG ratios generally around or below 1.5, and forward EPS growth exceeding 10%. Qualitatively, the screening weighted companies possessing durable economic moats—such as high switching costs, proprietary intellectual property, sole-source regulatory integration, or regional distribution scale. Macro themes informing selection include North American supply chain reshoring, power grid modernization, aging demographics, AI data center cooling infrastructure, and specialized automation solutions.

== 選股方法論 ==
本關注名單透過系統化篩選美國股票市場建立，旨在找出基本面優異的中小型複合成長股（市值介於 5 億至 500 億美元），同時剔除所有超大型科技巨頭、投機性動能股及網路熱門股。定量篩選條件要求具備正向自由現金流、可控的負債結構（多數產業的負債權益比低於 1.0）、預估 PEG 通常在 1.5 左右或以下，且預估每股盈餘（EPS）成長率超過 10%。定性方面，篩選機制重點考量具備持久經濟護城河的公司——例如高客戶轉換成本、專利知識產權、單一來源的監管整合或區域分銷規模。引導本次選股的宏觀主題包括北美供應鏈回流、電網現代化、人口老齡化、AI 資料中心冷卻基礎設施以及專用自動化解決方案。

== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening framework identifies non-mega-cap US-listed equities ($500M–$50B market capitalization) exhibiting strong fundamental compounder characteristics while avoiding hype-driven, media-trending, or momentum stocks. Securities were evaluated on a combination of quantitative and qualitative pillars: sustainable forward earnings growth, reasonable price-to-earnings relative to growth (PEG preferred below 1.5), manageable balance sheet leverage (low debt-to-equity), and durable free cash flow generation. Qualitative screens prioritized companies with clear competitive moats—such as proprietary technologies, municipal regulatory specifications, specialized market consolidation strategies, or high customer switching costs. Sector balance was intentionally maintained across Industrials, Healthcare, Financials, Technology, and Consumer sectors, favoring companies tied to secular macro themes (power grid modernization, thermal management for data centers, specialized insurance pricing, radiopharmaceuticals, and niche wholesale consolidation) while ignoring short-term market noise.

== 選股方法論 ==
本選股篩選架構旨在識別市值介於 5 億至 500 億美元之間、具備強勁基本面複利成長特質的中小型美股，同時避開市場過熱、媒體熱炒或動能型股票。個股透過定量與定性兩大支柱進行評估：預估未來的可持續盈餘成長率、相對成長性合理的本益比（PEG 優選小於 1.5）、可控的資產負債表槓桿（低負債權益比），以及強勁的自由現金流產生能力。定性篩選則優先考慮具有明確護城河的公司，例如專利技術、地方政府法規規範、專業領域整合策略或高客戶轉換成本。產業配置刻意保持在工業、醫療保健、金融、科技與消費等板塊之間均衡佈局，偏好與長期宏觀趨勢（電網升級、資料中心熱管理、特種保險定價權、核藥診斷及利基批發整合）相結合的公司，同時過濾掉市場的短期噪音。

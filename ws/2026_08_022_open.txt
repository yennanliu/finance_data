== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
We screened for US-listed companies with market caps between $500M and $50B to target the small/mid-cap sweet spot where institutional coverage is lower and mispricings are more common. We filtered out S&P 500 top-50 mega-caps and high-sentiment momentum stocks by excluding those with extreme retail trading volumes or excessive media coverage. Our quantitative filters prioritized consistent revenue and EPS growth, positive free cash flow, manageable leverage (Debt/Equity < 1.5), and attractive valuations relative to growth (PEG < 1.5). We focused on structural secular tailwinds—such as infrastructure modernization, advanced manufacturing reshoring, aging demographics, and specialized environmental services—where companies possess clear pricing power and proprietary competitive moats.

== 選股方法論 ==
我們篩選了市值在 5 億美元至 500 億美元之間的美國上市公司，以鎖定機構覆蓋率較低且定價偏差較常見的中小型股黃金區間。我們排除了標普 500 指數前 50 大巨型股以及高熱度的動能股，過濾掉散戶交易量極端或媒體過度報導的標的。我們的量化篩選優先考慮持續的營收和每股盈餘（EPS）成長、正向自由現金流、可控的槓桿比率（負債權益比 < 1.5），以及相對於成長性具吸引力的估值（本益成長比 PEG < 1.5）。我們專注於結構性長期趨勢——例如基礎設施現代化、先進製造業回流、人口老化以及專業環境服務——在這些領域中，公司擁有明確的定價能力和專有的競爭護城河。

== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening framework for this watchlist specifically filtered out mega-cap stocks (market caps exceeding $50B) and high-profile financial media "darlings" to avoid overhyped valuations and crowding risk. Instead, the methodology favored under-the-radar small-to-mid cap companies ($500M to $50B market cap) exhibiting structural competitive advantages, manageable leverage (low Debt/Equity), strong Free Cash Flow generation, and valuation discipline (target PEG < 1.5). Weighting was balanced between secular growth tailwinds (industrial reshoring, grid modernization, data center thermal management, smart infrastructure, aging demographics) and capital discipline (high return on invested capital and consistent balance sheet management). By selecting quality compounders operating in niche, essential industries, this portfolio seeks superior long-term fundamental risk-adjusted returns.

== 選股方法論 ==
本觀察名單的篩選架構特別過濾掉超大型股（市值超過 500 億美元）以及高調的財經媒體「寵兒」，以避免高估值風險與擁擠交易。相反地，本方法論偏好未被充分關注的中小型公司（市值介於 5 億至 500 億美元之間），這些公司展現出結構性競爭優勢、可控的槓桿率（低負債權益比）、強勁的自由現金流產生能力以及嚴格的估值紀律（目標本益成長比 PEG < 1.5）。權重配置在長期結構性成長順風（工業製造業回流、電網現代化、資料中心散熱管理、智慧基礎設施、老齡化人口）與資本紀律（高投入資本回報率與持續的資產負債表管理）之間取得平衡。透過選擇在關鍵細分領域營運的高品質複合成長公司，本投資組合旨在尋求卓越的長期基本面風險調整後報酬。

== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
This watchlist was constructed using a quantitative and qualitative screening process designed to isolate high-quality, under-the-radar U.S. equities. First, liquidity and market cap filters ($500M to $50B) excluded mega-cap tech giants, low-float micro-caps, and S&P 500 top-50 holdings. Second, stocks experiencing high social media momentum, speculative retail trading volume, or extreme valuation disconnects were filtered out. The remaining universe was screened for fundamental durability: forward EPS growth (>10%), manageable debt-to-equity ratios (<0.90 with preference for net cash balance sheets), positive free cash flow yields, and PEG ratios under 1.5. Finally, qualitative fundamental research prioritized companies benefiting from structural macro tailwinds—such as U.S. manufacturing reshoring, data center power/cooling demand, specialty insurance pricing power, and niche semiconductor packaging—where technical complexity or regulatory licensing creates high barriers to entry.

== 選股方法論 ==
本觀察名單採用量化與質化篩選程序，旨在找出高品質且未被市場過度關注的美國股票。首先，流動性與市值過濾器（5 億至 500 億美元）排除巨型科技巨頭、低流通量的微型股以及標普 500 前 50 大成分股。其次，過濾掉在社群媒體上高度熱門、散戶投機交易量過大或估值嚴重脫離基本面的股票。對剩餘的股票池進行基本面耐久性篩選：預估 EPS 成長率（>10%）、可控的負債權益比（<0.90，偏好淨現金資產負債表）、正的自由現金流殖利率，以及小於 1.5 的本益成長比（PEG）。最後，質化基本面研究優先考慮受益於結構性宏觀順風的公司——例如美國製造業回流、資料中心電力/冷卻需求、專項保險定價權以及分眾半導體封裝——在這些領域中，技術複雜性或監管許可構構築了極高進入壁壘。

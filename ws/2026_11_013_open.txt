== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
This watchlist was constructed through a strict quantitative and qualitative screening process designed to isolate high-quality, mid-to-large-cap compounders ($500M–$50B) while systematically filtering out mega-cap tech giants, viral momentum/meme stocks, and market darlings. Stocks were required to exhibit double-digit revenue or forward EPS growth potential alongside reasonable valuations, targeting a PEG ratio below 1.5 where feasible. Balance sheet integrity was prioritized by filtering for manageable net debt levels and sustainable positive free cash flow yields (>3–8%). Macro themes driving selection include U.S. industrial reshoring, data center cooling/power infrastructure, electrical grid modernization, aging demographic healthcare spending, and specialized technology/niche pricing monopolies.

== 選股方法論 ==
本觀察名單透過嚴格的定量與定性篩選流程建立，旨在找出優質的中大型複合成長公司（市值介於 5 億至 500 億美元之間），同時系統性地排除巨型科技巨頭、網路熱門/迷因股以及市場焦點股。入選股票須具備雙位數的營收或預估每股盈餘成長潛力，且估值合理（盡可能設定本益成長比 PEG 低於 1.5）。資產負債表的健全度被列為優先考量，要求負債水準可控且具備可持續的正自由現金流殖利率（>3%–8%）。推動選股的大趨勢包括：美國工業回流、資料中心冷卻與電力基礎設施、電網現代化、老齡化人口的醫療保健支出，以及具備特種技術或區域壟斷優勢的市場龍頭。

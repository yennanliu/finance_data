== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening process designed for this watchlist excludes mega-cap technology monopolies (market caps > $500B), high-beta momentum stocks featured regularly in retail trading forums, and speculative pre-profit entities. The selection framework prioritizes US-listed companies in the $500M to $50B market cap range that possess identifiable operational moats, low-to-moderate leverage (Debt/Equity generally < 1.0), positive Free Cash Flow yields, and attractive valuation growth profiles (PEG ratio target < 1.5). Portfolio weighting favors structural tailwinds including domestic industrial reshoring, grid modernization, healthcare automation, and supply chain digitalization. Companies chosen demonstrate durable pricing power and disciplined capital allocation strategies, serving as long-term compounders suitable for patient, value-oriented investors.

== 選股方法論 ==
本關注清單的篩選機制排除了巨型科技壟斷企業（市值高於 $5,000 億美元）、零售交易論壇中熱議的高貝塔動能股，以及投機性的未盈利實體。選股架構優先考慮市值介於 $5 億至 $500 億美元之間的美國上市公司，要求其具備明確的可識別營運護城河、低至中等槓桿率（負債權益比通常小於 1.0）、正向自由現金流殖利率，以及具吸引力的估值成長匹配度（目標本益成長比 PEG < 1.5）。投資組合權重偏向受益於結構性尾勁的領域，包括本土工業供應鏈回流、電網升級現代化、醫療自動化以及供應鏈數位化。獲選公司均展現出持久的定價能力與嚴格的資本配置策略，是適合耐心、價值導向型投資者的長期獲利複合成長標的。

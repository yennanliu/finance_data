== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The selection process focused on identifying US-listed companies with durable competitive advantages and strong, sustainable growth trajectories that are often overlooked by mainstream financial media or are not yet in the mega-cap spotlight. I filtered out S&P 500 top-50 constituents and any stock exhibiting meme-like or purely momentum-driven behavior. Emphasis was placed on companies benefiting from long-term structural tailwinds such as digital transformation, an aging population, decarbonization, and increasing cybersecurity needs. Valuation was considered through a PEG ratio lens (ideally below 1.5), balancing growth potential with a reasonable forward P/E, while also scrutinizing balance sheets for manageable debt and positive free cash flow generation. The target market cap sweet spot of $500M to $50B ensured a focus on mid-to-large cap companies with established operations but significant upside potential.

== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
This watchlist was constructed by applying a rigorous quantitative and qualitative screening framework focused on profitable, structural compounders outside the mega-cap spotlight. First, mega-cap tech stocks, media darlings, and speculative momentum plays were systematically excluded. Filter criteria prioritized small-to-mid cap US equities ($500M to $50B market cap) exhibiting forward revenue growth over 8%, double-digit forward EPS growth, and a price-to-earnings-to-growth (PEG) ratio generally below 1.5x. Balance sheet strength was emphasized by filtering for manageable debt leverage (Debt/Equity < 1.5) and positive free cash flow yields. Crucially, each company was evaluated for a distinct structural competitive moat—such as proprietary technology, non-discretionary regulatory demand, or high customer switching costs—and alignment with long-term macroeconomic tailwinds including grid modernization, industrial re-shoring, digitized market infrastructure, and health-tech innovation.

== 選股方法論 ==
本觀察名單建立於一套嚴謹的量化與質化篩選框架，專注於巨型股聚光燈之外具盈利能力與結構性複合成長的公司。首先，系統性地排除巨型科技股、媒體熱門股及投機性動能股。篩選條件優先考慮市值介於 5 億至 500 億美元之間的美國中小型股票，且預估營收成長率高於 8%、預估每股盈餘（EPS）實現雙位數成長，且本益成長比（PEG）普遍低於 1.5 倍。資產負債表的健康度受到高度重視，要求負債比率可控（負債權益比 < 1.5）並具備正向自由現金流殖利率。最重要的是，每家公司都經過評估，確保其具備明確的結構性競爭護城河（如專利技術、非選擇性法規需求或高客戶轉換成本），並契合長期宏觀尾風，包括電網現代化、工業本土重構、金融市場基礎設施數位化及醫療科技創新。

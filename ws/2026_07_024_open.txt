== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
Our screening process systematically filters out mega-cap stocks (S&P 500 top 50) and high-beta, media-hyped names to focus on the sweet spot of small-to-mid-cap compounders ($500M to $50B). We prioritize companies with a PEG ratio under 1.5, indicating growth priced at a reasonable valuation, and strong balance sheets characterized by low debt and robust free cash flow. Sector selection is heavily informed by structural, non-cyclical tailwinds such as domestic manufacturing reshoring, water infrastructure modernization, advanced automation, and specialized healthcare services. By focusing on niche leaders with high switching costs and regulatory or technological moats, this watchlist identifies resilient compounders capable of delivering long-term alpha.

== 選股方法論 ==
我們的篩選流程系統性地過濾了超大型股（標普 500 指數前 50 大）以及高貝塔值、媒體過度炒作的股票，以專注於中小型複利成長股（市值 5 億至 500 億美元）的黃金區間。我們優先考慮本益成長比（PEG）低於 1.5 的公司，這代表其成長性具有合理的估值，同時也看重具備低負債與強勁自由現金流的穩健資產負債表。產業選擇深受結構性、非週期性順風的影響，例如本土製造業回流、水資源基礎設施現代化、先進自動化以及專業醫療保健服務。透過專注於具有高轉換成本、法規或技術護城河的利基市場領導者，此觀察清單識別出了能夠提供長期超額收益（Alpha）的韌性複利企業。

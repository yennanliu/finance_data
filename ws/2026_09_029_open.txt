== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
Our screening process focused on identifying high-quality, mid-cap compounders ($500M to $50B market cap) that operate outside the spotlight of mainstream financial media. We filtered out mega-caps, high-beta momentum stocks, and unprofitable speculative plays. Instead, we prioritized companies with a proven track record of positive free cash flow, low-to-moderate leverage, and a clear competitive moat—such as high switching costs, regulatory barriers, or proprietary technology. We heavily weighted reasonable valuations relative to growth (targeting a PEG ratio under 1.5) and focused on secular macro themes including domestic industrial reshoring, infrastructure modernization, water resource management, and specialized B2B software.

== 選股方法論 ==
我們的篩選過程專注於尋找運營在主流財經媒體聚光燈之外的高品質、中型股複利機器（市值介於 5 億至 500 億美元之間）。我們過濾掉了超大型股、高貝塔值的動能股以及無獲利的投機性股票。相反地，我們優先考慮那些在自由現金流、低至中度槓桿以及明確競爭護城河（如高轉換成本、監管壁壘或專有技術）方面擁有良好紀錄的公司。我們高度重視相對於成長性而言合理的估值（目標本益成長比 PEG 低於 1.5），並專注於長期的宏觀主題，包括國內工業回流、基礎設施現代化、水資源管理以及專業的 B2B 軟體。

== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
This watchlist was constructed by filtering out the top 50 S&P 500 companies, high-beta momentum stocks, and high-profile media darlings to focus strictly on under-the-radar compounders. We weighted growth against value by targeting companies with strong structural tailwinds (such as infrastructure reshoring, water conservation, advanced semiconductor packaging, and specialized healthcare) that are trading at reasonable valuations, specifically prioritizing a PEG ratio below 1.5. Balance sheet health was prioritized by selecting companies with low debt-to-equity ratios and robust free cash flow yields. This methodology identifies resilient businesses with high-barrier-to-entry moats that are positioned to compound capital patiently over a long-term horizon.

== 選股方法論 ==
本觀察名單的構建首先過濾掉了標普 500 指數前 50 大企業、高貝塔值的動能股以及高調的媒體寵兒，以便嚴格專注於未受市場過度關注的優質複利企業。我們在成長與價值之間進行權衡，鎖定具有強大結構性順風（如基礎設施回流、水資源保護、先進半導體封裝和專業醫療保健）且估值合理的公司，並特別優先考慮本益成長比（PEG）低於 1.5 的標的。在資產負債表健康狀況方面，我們優先選擇負債權益比低且自由現金流殖利率強勁的公司。此方法論旨在識別具有高進入壁壘護城河的韌性企業，使其能夠在長期內耐心地實現資本複利。

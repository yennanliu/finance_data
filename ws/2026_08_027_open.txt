== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
This watchlist was constructed using a multi-stage fundamental screen designed to filter out market noise, speculative momentum, and overvalued mega-caps. First, we excluded all companies with market caps exceeding $50 billion or those featured heavily in momentum-driven retail trading forums. We prioritized companies with a PEG ratio below 1.5, ensuring that we are not overpaying for growth. Balance sheet health was heavily weighted, requiring low debt-to-equity ratios and positive free cash flow yields. Finally, we qualitative-screened for structural tailwinds—such as grid modernization, infrastructure spending, aging demographics, and automation—and verified that each business possesses a clear, defensible competitive moat (e.g., high switching costs, regulatory barriers, or proprietary technology) to ensure long-term compounding potential.

== 選股方法論 ==
本觀察名單採用多階段基本面篩選法構建，旨在過濾市場噪音、投機性動能和估值過高的巨型股。首先，我們排除了所有市值超過 500 億美元的公司，以及在動能驅動的散戶交易論壇中頻繁出現的公司。我們優先考慮本益成長比（PEG）低於 1.5 的公司，以確保我們沒有為成長支付過高溢價。資產負債表的健康狀況被賦予了極大權重，要求低負債權益比和正的自由現金流殖利率。最後，我們對結構性利多（如電網現代化、基礎設施支出、人口老化和自動化）進行了質化篩選，並確認每家企業都擁有清晰、可防守的競爭護城河（例如高轉換成本、監管壁壘或專有技術），以確保長期的複利潛力。

== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
Our screening logic is designed to systematically eliminate market hype and speculative momentum while uncovering hidden champions with robust fundamental backing. We first excluded the top 50 S&P 500 mega-caps and filtered out stocks with high retail social media sentiment or excessive media coverage. In weighting growth versus value, we prioritized companies with high return on invested capital (ROIC), consistent positive free cash flow, and a PEG ratio generally below 1.5, ensuring we do not buy growth at an unreasonable price. This list is heavily informed by multi-year secular themes: the re-shoring and automation of North American manufacturing, critical water and environmental infrastructure upgrades, decentralized defense systems, and high-barrier niche markets in healthcare.

== 選股方法論 ==
我們的篩選邏輯旨在系統性地排除市場熱門股和投機性動能股，同時發掘具有強大基本面支撐的隱形冠軍。我們首先排除了標普 500 指數市值前 50 大的巨頭，並過濾掉社交媒體散戶熱議或媒體高度曝光的標的。在權衡成長與價值時，我們優先考慮具備高投入資本回報率（ROIC）、持續產生正向自由現金流且本益成長比（PEG）通常低於 1.5 的企業，以確保不以過高價格買入成長。本名單深受多個長期宏觀主題的啟發，包括北美製造業回流與自動化、水資源與環境基礎設施的迫切升級、國防系統的去中心化，以及醫療保健領域中高壁壘的利基市場。

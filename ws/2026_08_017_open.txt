== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening logic for this watchlist was designed to filter out short-term market noise and identify high-quality, under-the-radar compounders. We excluded mega-cap stocks (S&P 500 top-50) and highly discussed momentum/meme stocks to focus on the small-to-mid-cap sweet spot ($500M to $50B). We prioritized companies with strong balance sheets (low debt-to-equity and robust free cash flow yields) and reasonable valuations relative to their growth trajectories (targeting a PEG ratio under 1.5). Weight was heavily given to businesses with clear, structural competitive moats—such as high switching costs, proprietary technology, or oligopolistic market structures—operating under long-term tailwinds like infrastructure modernization, reshoring, and demographic shifts.

== 選股方法論 ==
此觀察名單的篩選邏輯旨在過濾掉市場的短期噪音，並識別出高質量、被低估的長期複利成長股。我們排除了超大型股（標普 500 指數前 50 大企業）以及市場熱議的動能股/迷因股，以專注於中小型股的黃金區間（市值介於 5 億至 500 億美元）。我們優先考慮資產負債表強健（低負債權益比和穩健的自由現金流殖利率）且估值相對於其成長軌跡合理的公司（目標本益成長比 PEG 低於 1.5）。選股權重高度向具備明確、結構性競爭護城河（如高轉換成本、專有技術或寡占市場結構）的企業傾斜，且這些企業需營運於基礎設施現代化、製造業回流和人口結構轉變等長期順風之下。

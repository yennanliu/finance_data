== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
This watchlist was constructed through a rigorous fundamental screening process focused on US-listed small- and mid-cap equities ($500M–$50B market cap). To remove hype and market darlings, all S&P 500 top-50 mega-caps, high-beta momentum stocks, and media-hyped names were excluded. The screening prioritized: (1) fundamental growth criteria, targeting revenue growth, double-digit forward EPS growth, and reasonable valuations (targeting PEG < 1.5 where possible); (2) balance sheet durability, favoring zero-to-low net debt and robust free cash flow yields; and (3) structural competitive moats, such as proprietary technology, regulatory barriers, high customer switching costs, or long-term tailwinds (e.g., grid modernization, industrial automation, re-shoring, and specialized healthcare). Valuation was weighted equally with quality to ensure long-term downside protection while participating in structural growth.

== 選股方法論 ==
本觀察名單透過針對美國上市中小盤股（市值介於 5 億至 500 億美元）的嚴謹基本面篩選流程建構。為排除炒作與市場寵兒，所有標普 500 前 50 大巨型股、高 Beta 動能股及媒體熱炒標的均被排除。篩選重點在於：(1) 基本面成長指標，目標為健康的營收成長、雙位數的預估 EPS 成長及合理的估值（盡可能尋求 PEG < 1.5）；(2) 資產負債表的耐久性，偏好零或低淨負債以及強勁的自由現金流殖利率；(3) 結構性競爭護城河，如專利技術、法規壁壘、高客戶轉換成本或長期產業順風（例如電網現代化、工業自動化、供應鏈回流及專業醫療保健）。估值與企業品質賦予同等權重，以確保在參與結構性成長的同時獲得長期的下行保護。

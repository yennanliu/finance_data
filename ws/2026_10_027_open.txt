== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
This fundamental screening logic targets resilient, mid- and small-cap US businesses ($500M–$50B) with high structural barriers to entry while excluding mega-cap tech giants, hyper-volatile momentum trades, and popular media darlings. Qualitative filters favor companies with clear competitive moats—such as high switching costs, proprietary engineering standards, or dominant regional footprints—operating within long-term tailwinds like electrical grid modernization, industrial reshoring, infrastructure maintenance, and specialized healthcare solutions. Quantitatively, candidate selection requires solid balance sheets (low debt/equity), strong positive free cash flow yields, and durable profit expansion. Growth and valuation are balanced by prioritizing companies trading at attractive Price-to-Earnings-to-Growth (PEG < 1.5) ratios, offering investors an optimal combination of business quality, margin safety, and long-term earnings compounding.

== 選股方法論 ==
本基本面篩選邏輯旨在鎖定具備高結構性進入門檻且具韌性的美國中小型企業（市值 5 億至 500 億美元），同時排除巨型科技巨頭、高波動的動能交易股以及主流媒體熱門股。定性篩選偏好擁有明確競爭護城河（如高轉換成本、專有工程標準或主導性的區域版圖）的公司，並處於電網現代化、工業製造回流、基礎設施維護及專業醫療保健解決方案等長期順風趨勢中。定量方面，候選標的需具備健全資產負債表（低負債權益比）、強勁的正自由現金流殖利率及持久的盈餘擴張能力。在成長與估值之間取得平衡，優先選擇交易於具吸引力本益成長比（PEG < 1.5）的公司，為投資人提供商業品質、邊際安全與長期盈餘複合成長的最佳組合。

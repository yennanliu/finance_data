== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
Our screening process systematically filtered out mega-cap stocks (market cap > $50B) and high-beta momentum names that dominate mainstream financial media headlines. We prioritized companies with market caps between $500M and $50B, focusing on the small-to-mid-cap sweet spot where market inefficiencies are most common. The core quantitative filter required positive free cash flow, manageable debt-to-equity ratios, and a forward PEG ratio ideally below 1.5, balancing growth potential with valuation discipline. Qualitatively, we selected companies possessing clear structural tailwinds—such as water infrastructure upgrades, advanced semiconductor packaging, environmental regulation, and demographic shifts—coupled with high switching costs or proprietary technology that form durable competitive moats. This methodology yields a highly diversified, resilient watchlist of overlooked compounders built for long-term capital appreciation.

== 選股方法論 ==
我們的篩選過程系統性地過濾掉了大型股（市值 > $50B）以及主導主流財經媒體頭條的高貝塔值動能股。我們優先考慮市值在 $500M 至 $50B 之間的非熱門股，專注於市場效率最常失效的中小型股黃金地帶。核心量化篩選要求具備正向自由現金流、可控的負債權益比，且預估本益成長比（PEG）理想情況下低於 1.5，從而在成長潛力與估值紀律之間取得平衡。在質化方面，我們選擇了具備明確結構性順風（如水務基礎設施升級、先進半導體封裝、環境監管和人口結構轉變）的公司，並結合了高轉換成本或專有技術，以形成持久的競爭護城河。這種方法論產生了一個高度多元化、具備韌性的被忽視複利型企業觀察名單，專為長期資本增值而構建。

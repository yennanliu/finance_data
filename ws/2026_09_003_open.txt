== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The selection process focused on identifying high-quality, mid-to-small-cap companies ($500M–$50B) that are largely ignored by mainstream financial media and retail momentum traders. To filter out overhyped stocks, we excluded any S&P 500 top-50 constituents and companies with unsustainably high valuations (PEG > 2.5 was generally avoided unless justified by an exceptional, near-monopolistic moat). We prioritized companies with strong balance sheets (low debt-to-equity ratios) and robust free cash flow yields, ensuring they can fund their own growth without relying on expensive capital markets. The selection is heavily informed by long-term structural macro themes, including industrial reshoring, data center infrastructure expansion, water scarcity, grid modernization, and aging demographics. By focusing on niche market leaders with high switching costs and regulatory barriers, this watchlist represents a patient, value-oriented approach to capturing secular compounding growth.

== 選股方法論 ==
選股過程專注於識別基本面優良、且在很大程度上被主流財經媒體和散戶動能交易者忽視的中小型公司（市值在 $500M 至 $50B 之間）。為了過濾掉過度炒作的股票，我們排除了任何標普 500 指數前 50 大成分股以及估值過高（通常避免本益成長比 PEG > 2.5，除非有極其獨特、接近壟斷的護城河支持）的公司。我們優先考慮資產負債表強健（負債權益比低）且自由現金流殖利率強勁的公司，以確保它們能夠在不依賴高成本資本市場的情況下為自身成長提供資金。此選股名單高度結合了長期的結構性宏觀主題，包括工業回流、數據中心基礎設施擴張、水資源短缺、電網現代化以及人口老齡化。通過專注於具有高轉換成本和監管壁壘的利基市場領導者，本觀察名單代表了一種耐心、價值導向的方法，旨在捕捉長期的結構性複利成長。

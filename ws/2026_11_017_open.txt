== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
This watchlist was constructed through a disciplined fundamental screening process designed to isolate high-quality, under-followed US equities while strictly excluding mega-cap index leaders, viral momentum stocks, and media darlings. Screened companies were filtered for market capitalization between $500M and $50B, low-to-moderate leverage, positive free cash flow generation, and reasonable valuations (targeting PEG ratios below 1.5). Fundamental weighting prioritized structural competitive moats—such as high switching costs, specialized technical IP, localized oligopolies, and high return on invested capital—over short-term momentum. Sector allocation deliberately emphasizes structural macro trends including US manufacturing reshoring, power grid and infrastructure modernization, specialty insurance pricing resilience, and niche healthcare/biotech services, offering long-term value investors compounding capital growth potential.

== 選股方法論 ==
本觀察名單透過嚴謹的基本面篩選程序建立，旨在找出具備高品質且未被市場充分關注的美國股票，同時嚴格排除超大型市值指數龍頭、病毒式動能股與媒體寵兒。篩選條件包含市值介於 5 億美元至 500 億美元之間、低至中度槓桿、具備正向自由現金流產生能力，以及合理的估值（目標本益成長比 PEG 低於 1.5）。基本面權重優先考量結構性經濟護城河——如高轉換成本、專利的技術智慧財產權、在地化寡頭壟斷與高投入資本回報率——而非短期動能。產業配置重點聚焦於結構性宏觀趨勢，包括美國製造業回流、電網與基礎設施現代化、特種險定價韌性，以及利基型醫療保健/生技服務，為長期價值投資人提供資本長期複利成長的潛力。

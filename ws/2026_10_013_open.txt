== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
This watchlist was constructed through a disciplined multi-factor quantitative and fundamental screening strategy designed to identify high-quality, long-term secular winners while strictly excluding market-darlings, meme momentum plays, and S&P 500 mega-caps. The process first filtered the US equity universe for market capitalizations between $500M and $50B. Next, quantitative screens enforced double-digit EPS growth trajectories, solid return on capital profiles, positive free cash flow, and manageable leverage profiles (typically Debt/Equity < 1.0), with preference given to stocks trading at a PEG ratio below 1.5. Quantitative outputs were then subjected to qualitative fundamental research focused on economic moats—such as high switching costs, regulatory barriers, scale advantages, or proprietary technology. Macroeconomic themes informing this selection include power grid modernization and industrial electrification, localized supply chain reshoring, infrastructure services, biopharma processing tools, and high-margin recurring cash flow business models.

== 選股方法論 ==
本觀察名單採用紀律嚴明的多因子定量與基本面篩選策略構建，旨在識別高品質的長期結構性贏家，同時嚴格排除市場熱門股、迷因動能股和標普500超大型股。篩選程序首先將美股市場範圍縮小至市值在 5 億美元至 500 億美元之間的標的。接者，定量篩選要求具備雙位數的每股盈餘成長軌跡、穩健的資本回報率、正向的自由現金流以及可控的負債水平（通常負債權益比 < 1.0），並優先考慮本益成長比（PEG）低於 1.5 的股票。定量篩選結果進一步接受專注於經濟護城河的高質性基本面研究，評估如高轉換成本、法規壁壘、規模優勢或獨家技術等要素。主導本次選股的宏觀經濟主題包括：電網現代化與工業電氣化、在地化供應鏈回流、基礎設施服務、生物製藥加工工具，以及高毛利經常性現金流商業模式。

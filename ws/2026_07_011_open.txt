== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The selection process focused on identifying US-listed companies with durable competitive advantages operating in sectors benefiting from long-term structural tailwinds, while deliberately avoiding mega-caps, media darlings, or momentum plays. Screening prioritized market caps between $500M and $50B, with a preference for the lower end of that range to maximize "under-the-radar" potential. Financial health was paramount, looking for manageable debt and a clear path to positive free cash flow, indicating operational strength and self-funding capacity. Growth was assessed through a combination of projected revenue and EPS growth, with a strong emphasis on valuation discipline (PEG < 1.5 preferred, though some high-quality moats justified slightly higher). Macro themes like digital transformation (B2B software, data analytics), healthcare innovation (non-pharma), water/environmental infrastructure, and specialized industrial technology heavily informed the picks, seeking businesses with essential products or services that are less susceptible to discretionary spending cuts.

== 選股方法論 ==
本次選股流程著重於識別在受益於長期結構性趨勢的產業中營運、具有持久競爭優勢的美國上市公司，同時刻意避開大型股、媒體寵兒或動能股。篩選優先考慮市值介於5億美元至500億美元之間的公司，並傾向於該範圍的較低端，以最大限度地發掘「被低估」的潛力。財務健康至關重要，我們尋求債務可控且有明確途徑實現正自由現金流的公司，這表明其營運實力及自我融資能力。成長評估結合了預期營收和每股盈餘成長，並高度重視估值紀律（本益成長比優選低於1.5，儘管一些高品質護城河公司允許略高）。數位轉型（B2B軟體、數據分析）、醫療保健創新（非製藥）、水/環境基礎設施以及專業工業技術等宏觀主題，是本次選股的重要參考依據，旨在尋找那些基本產品或服務不易受非必要支出削減影響的企業。

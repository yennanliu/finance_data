== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
This watchlist was constructed using a fundamental stock screening process designed to uncover high-quality, under-the-radar US mid-cap and non-mega-cap compounders. Market capitalization was constrained primarily to the $500M–$50B window, strictly excluding S&P 500 top-50 mega-caps, high-beta momentum stocks, and media darlings. Quantitative filters prioritized stable revenue growth (>8% YoY), solid forward EPS acceleration, comfortable debt ratios (Debt/Equity under 1.0 for non-financials), and positive free cash flow yields with PEG ratios ideally below 1.5. Qualitatively, preference was given to companies exhibiting structural moats—such as proprietary technology, regulatory insertion, network effects, or high customer switching costs—and alignment with secular tailwinds like grid modernization, AI cooling infrastructure, niche medical technology, and market consolidation.

== 選股方法論 ==
本觀察名單採用基本面股票篩選流程建構，旨在挖掘優質、未被市場過度關注的美國中型股與非超大型複合成長企業。市值主要限制在 5 億美元至 500 億美元的區間內，嚴格排除標普 500 指數前 50 大巨型股、高 Beta 值的動能股與媒體熱門股。定量篩選優先考慮穩健的營收成長（年增率 >8%）、強勁的預估每股盈餘加速成長、健康的負債比率（非金融業負債權益比低於 1.0），以及具備正向自由現金流殖利率且本益成長比（PEG）理想上低於 1.5 的企業。定性方面，優先選擇展現出結構性競爭護城河的公司（如專有技術、法規嵌入、網路效應或高客戶轉換成本），並契合長期大趨勢（如電網現代化、AI 液冷基礎設施、特種醫療技術及產業整併）。

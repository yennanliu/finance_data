== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening logic was designed to filter out the noise of highly publicized mega-caps and momentum-driven market favorites, focusing instead on high-quality, mid-and-small-cap compounders. We excluded companies in the S&P 500 top-50 and those with excessive retail media sentiment. Candidates were screened for robust fundamental health: consistent positive free cash flow, manageable leverage (typically Debt/Equity < 1.0, with exceptions for asset-heavy businesses with contracted cash flows), and a favorable valuation relative to growth (targeting PEG < 1.5). We prioritized companies with clear structural tailwinds—such as infrastructure modernization, localized manufacturing nearshoring, demographic aging, and specialized technological niches—that possess high switching costs or regional oligopolies to protect their margins.

== 選股方法論 ==
篩選邏輯旨在排除高調的超大型股和動能驅動的熱門市場寵兒，轉而專注於高品質、中小型市值的複利成長股。我們排除了標普 500 指數前 50 大公司以及零售媒體情緒過熱的企業。候選公司經過嚴格的財務健康狀況篩選：持續的自由現金流、可控的槓桿率（通常負債權益比 < 1.0，具有合約現金流的資產密集型業務除外），以及相對於成長性而言具備吸引力的估值（目標本益成長比 PEG < 1.5）。我們優先選擇具有明確結構性順風（如基礎設施現代化、本土化製造業回流、人口老化和專業技術利基）且擁有高轉換成本或區域寡頭壟斷以保護其利潤率的公司。

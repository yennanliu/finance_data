== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening logic prioritized companies demonstrating a clear, durable competitive moat or strong structural tailwind, ensuring long-term relevance and pricing power. To avoid "market media darlings" and mega-caps, I explicitly filtered out the top 50 S&P 500 companies by market cap and avoided any stock frequently highlighted in mainstream financial news or showing characteristics of speculative momentum. Growth was balanced with value, favoring companies with double-digit revenue and EPS growth, but with a strong preference for a PEG ratio below 1.5, indicating that growth is not excessively priced in. Balance sheet strength, evidenced by manageable debt (Debt/Equity generally below 1.0, with exceptions for highly cash-generative businesses) and robust free cash flow generation, was a critical filter for financial resilience. Macro and sector themes informing the picks included digital transformation (SaaS, FinTech, cloud infrastructure), aging demographics and healthcare innovation, infrastructure modernization, industrial automation, clean energy transition, and resilient supply chains. The market cap sweet spot of $500M-$50B was strictly adhered to, targeting the small-to-mid cap space where under-the-radar opportunities are more prevalent.

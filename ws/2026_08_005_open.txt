== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
Our screening process systematically filters out mega-cap stocks (market cap > $50B) and high-beta, media-hyped names by focusing on companies with low institutional crowding and moderate trading volumes. We prioritize fundamental quality, weighting consistent historical and forward earnings growth alongside high free cash flow generation and low debt-to-equity ratios. We target a PEG ratio below 1.5 to ensure growth is purchased at a reasonable price. The selection is heavily informed by durable secular themes, including infrastructure modernization, localized manufacturing re-shoring, water scarcity, advanced medical technologies, and automation, ensuring each business possesses a structural competitive moat.

== 選股方法論 ==
我們的篩選流程系統性地過濾掉超大型股（市值大於 500 億美元）以及高貝塔值、媒體過度炒作的股票，轉而專注於機構持股集中度低且交易量適中的公司。我們優先考慮基本面品質，將持續的歷史和預估盈餘成長，與高自由現金流產生能力及低負債權益比相結合。我們將 PEG 比率控制在 1.5 以下，以確保以合理的價格購買成長。選股深受持久的結構性主題啟發，包括基礎設施現代化、在地化製造業回流、水資源短缺、先進醫療技術和自動化，確保每家企業都具備結構性的競爭護城河。

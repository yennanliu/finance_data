== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
This screening process was designed to identify high-quality, under-the-radar compounders while systematically filtering out mega-caps, meme stocks, and overhyped market darlings. The screen targeted US-listed companies with market caps between $500M and $50B, requiring a forward PEG ratio under 1.5 to ensure growth is purchased at a reasonable price. Financial health was prioritized by filtering for positive free cash flow, high FCF yields, and manageable debt-to-equity ratios. The selection heavily weighted companies operating in niches with high structural barriers to entry—such as regulatory protections (e.g., the Jones Act, environmental permitting), proprietary technology (e.g., drug delivery patents), or high switching costs. Key macro themes informing these picks include US infrastructure modernization, aging demographics, and industrial reshoring.

== 選股方法論 ==
本選股流程旨在識別高質量、未受市場關注的複利型企業，同時系統性地過濾掉超大市值股、迷因股和過度炒作的市場寵兒。篩選對象為市值在 5 億美元至 500 億美元之間的美國上市公司，並要求預估本益成長比（PEG）低於 1.5，以確保能以合理價格買入成長。我們通過篩選正向自由現金流、高自由現金流殖利率和可控的負債權益比，將財務健康狀況放在首位。選股時重點權衡了那些在具有高結構性進入壁壘的利基市場中營運的公司——例如監管保護（如《瓊斯法案》、環境許可證）、專利技術（如藥物遞送專利）或高轉換成本。啟發這些選擇的核心宏觀主題包括美國基礎設施現代化、人口老化以及製造業回流。

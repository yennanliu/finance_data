== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
Our screening process actively filters out mega-cap stocks and highly discussed "media darlings" by excluding any company in the top 100 of the S&P 500 or those with extreme retail trading volume and social media sentiment scores. We prioritize companies with a market capitalization between $500 million and $50 billion that exhibit high-quality fundamental characteristics: consistent double-digit revenue and earnings growth, high free cash flow conversion, and a PEG ratio ideally below 1.5. We heavily weight structural tailwinds—such as domestic manufacturing reshoring, infrastructure modernization, water conservation, and demographic aging—combined with high switching costs or regulatory moats. This value-oriented, patient approach ensures we identify resilient compounders that are mispriced or overlooked by short-term focused market participants.

== 選股方法論 ==
我們的篩選過程通過排除標準普爾 500 指數前 100 名的任何公司，或具有極端散戶交易量和社群媒體情緒評分的公司，積極過濾掉大盤股和高度討論的「媒體寵兒」。我們優先考慮市值在 5 億美元至 500 億美元之間、且展現出高質量基本面特徵的公司：持續的兩位數營收和盈餘成長、高自由現金流轉換率，以及本益成長比（PEG）理想情況下低於 1.5。我們高度重視結構性順風——例如國內製造業回流、基礎設施現代化、水資源保護和人口老齡化——並結合高轉換成本或法規護城河。這種以價值為導向、耐心的投資方法確保我們能夠識別出被短期關注的市場參與者錯誤定價或忽視的具彈性複利機器。

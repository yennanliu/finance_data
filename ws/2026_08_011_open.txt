== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening process was designed to identify high-quality, resilient compounders while strictly avoiding mega-cap tech, speculative meme stocks, and overhyped market darlings. We filtered the US investable universe for companies with market caps between $500 million and $50 billion, eliminating S&P 500 top-50 constituents. We prioritized businesses with robust balance sheets (low debt-to-equity ratios or net cash positions) and consistent positive free cash flow generation. Growth and valuation were balanced by targeting companies with a forward PEG ratio ideally under 1.5, ensuring we did not overpay for growth. The selection heavily favored companies benefiting from powerful, multi-year structural tailwinds—such as the nearshoring of manufacturing, water infrastructure modernization, aging demographics, and advanced industrial automation—where the business possesses a clear, defensible competitive moat.

== 選股方法論 ==
本篩選流程旨在識別高質量、具韌性的複利型企業，同時嚴格避開超大型科技股、投機性迷因股以及過度炒作的市場寵兒。我們在美國可投資市場中篩選市值介於 5 億美元至 500 億美元之間的企業，排除了標普 500 指數前 50 大成分股。我們優先考慮具有穩健資產負債表（低負債權益比或淨現金狀態）和持續產生正向自由現金流的企業。在成長性與估值之間取得平衡，目標是預估本益成長比（PEG）理想情況下低於 1.5，以確保我們不會為成長支付過高溢價。選股高度偏好受益於強大且具備多年度結構性利多的公司——例如製造業近岸外包、水資源基礎設施現代化、人口老化以及先進工業自動化——且該企業在其中擁有明確、可防守的競爭護城河。

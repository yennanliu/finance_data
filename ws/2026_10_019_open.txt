== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening process prioritizes quality growth at a reasonable price (GARP) among under-the-radar US equities while explicitly excluding the top 50 S&P 500 mega-caps, momentum/meme stocks, and media darlings. The universe was constrained to companies with market caps between $500 million and $50 billion demonstrating structural competitive advantages (economic moats such as intellectual property, high switching costs, or local monopolies), strong balance sheets (Debt/Equity typically under 0.80), and sustainable free cash flow generation. Valuation parameters filtered for forward PEG ratios near or below 1.5, balancing fundamental revenue and earnings trajectories against realistic market multiples. The final ranking incorporates sector diversification and secular macro trends, including grid electrification, healthcare demographic expansion, industrial automation, and US infrastructure reinvestment.

== 選股方法論 ==
本選股篩選程序優先考慮不受市場關注的美股中「合理價格的高質量成長（GARP）」標的，同時明確排除標普 500 前 50 大巨型科技股、高動能/迷因股以及媒體焦點熱門股。選股池限制在市值介於 5 億美元至 500 億美元之間、展現結構性競爭優勢（如智慧財產權、高轉用成本或區域壟斷等經濟護城河）、資產負債表穩健（負債權益比通常低於 0.80）且具備可持續自由現金流生成能力的企業。估值參數篩選預估本益成長比（PEG）接近或低於 1.5 的標的，平衡基本面營收與盈餘軌跡與合理的市場估值倍數。最終排名結合了產業分散性與長期宏觀趨勢，包括電網電氣化、醫療保健人口結構擴展、工業自動化以及美國基礎設施再投資。

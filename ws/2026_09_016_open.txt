== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening logic for this watchlist was designed to filter out the noise of mainstream financial media and identify high-quality, under-the-radar compounders. First, we excluded all companies with a market cap exceeding $50B or those featured in the S&P 500 top-50 to eliminate mega-cap bias. Second, we filtered out high-beta, momentum-driven stocks and meme names by applying strict valuation and balance sheet constraints, specifically targeting companies with a PEG ratio below 1.5 and manageable debt-to-equity ratios. We weighted consistent earnings growth and high free cash flow yield over speculative revenue-only growth. The selection is heavily informed by structural macroeconomic themes: the reshoring of US manufacturing, infrastructure upgrades (water and grid modernization), demographic shifts (aging population driving healthcare services), and niche technology enablers (optical packaging and data security). This ensures each pick is supported by a long-term secular tailwind rather than short-term market hype.

== 選股方法論 ==
本觀察名單的篩選邏輯旨在過濾掉主流財經媒體的噪音，並識別出高質量、未受關注的長期複利增長股。首先，我們排除了所有市值超過 500 億美元的公司或標普 500 指數前 50 大公司，以消除超大型股的偏差。其次，我們通過應用嚴格的估值和資產負債表限制（特別針對本益成長比 (PEG) 低於 1.5 且負債權益比可控的公司），過濾掉了高 Beta、動能驅動的股票和迷因股。相比於投機性的純營收增長，我們更看重持續的盈餘增長和高自由現金流殖利率。選股深受結構性宏觀經濟主題的啟發：美國製造業回流、基礎設施升級（水資源和電網現代化）、人口結構轉變（老齡化人口推動醫療服務）以及利基技術推動者（光學封裝和數據安全）。這確保了每個選擇都得到長期行業順風的支持，而非短期市場炒作。

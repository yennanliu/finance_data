== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening logic prioritized high-quality, mid-sized businesses with structural tailwinds that are insulated from short-term market hype. We filtered out mega-caps (market cap > $50B) and highly discussed momentum stocks to focus on overlooked compounders. Our quantitative filters required positive free cash flow, manageable debt (Debt/Equity < 1.5), and a reasonable valuation relative to growth (PEG < 1.5 preferred). We weighted structural tailwinds—such as industrial reshoring, smart infrastructure upgrades, healthcare demographics, and automation—more heavily than short-term cyclical trends, ensuring these companies possess durable competitive moats capable of compounding capital over a multi-year horizon.

== 選股方法論 ==
選股邏輯優先考慮具有結構性順風且不受短期市場炒作影響的高質量、中型企業。我們過濾掉了超大市值股（市值 > 500億美元）和高度討論的動能股，以專注於被忽視的複利企業。我們的定量篩選要求正的自由現金流、可控的債務（負債權益比 < 1.5）以及相對於成長的合理估值（本益成長比 PEG < 1.5 優先）。我們對結構性順風（如工業回流、智慧基礎設施升級、醫療人口統計和自動化）的權重高於短期週期性趨勢，以確保這些公司擁有持久的競爭護城河，能夠在多年期內實現資本複利。

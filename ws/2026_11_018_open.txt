== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening framework prioritizes fundamental compounders operating within the small-to-mid-cap sweet spot ($500M–$50B market cap), specifically targeting businesses overlooked by mainstream media and retail momentum channels. To exclude speculative or overhyped stocks, the screen filtered out any company in the top 50 S&P 500 market cap rankings, as well as those trading at disconnected growth valuations (PEG > 2.5 except where exceptional capital efficiency and moat quality justified it). Weighting favored high return on invested capital (ROIC), durable balance sheets (low Debt/Equity or net cash), expanding operating margins, and organic revenue growth supported by secular tailwinds (e.g., US industrial re-shoring, AI hardware interconnects, specialty insurance pricing dynamics, aging demographics, and power grid infrastructure).

== 選股方法論 ==
本篩選架構優先考慮在中小市值黃金區間（市值約 5 億美元至 500 億美元）內營運的基礎面複利型企業，特別針對被主流媒體與散戶動能熱點所忽視的公司。為排除投機性或過度炒作的股票，本篩選過濾掉標普 500 指數前 50 大市值的任何公司，以及成長估值過度脫節的公司（PEG > 2.5，除非其擁有極其卓越的資本效率與護城河品質）。權重偏好高投入資本回報率（ROIC）、穩健的資產負債表（低負債權益比或淨現金）、營業利益率持續擴張，以及受惠於長期結構性尾風（如美國工業製造業回流、AI 硬體光電互連、特種保險定價動能、人口老齡化與電網基礎建設升級）的內生性營收成長。

== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
This watchlist was constructed through a multi-stage fundamental screening process designed to isolate high-quality mid- and small-cap US equities while excluding short-term momentum favorites and mega-cap tech stocks. First, the screen excluded all components of the S&P 500 top-50, companies with market capitalizations exceeding $50B, and stocks driven primarily by retail momentum or social media sentiment. Second, companies were filtered for fundamental strength: positive free cash flow, manageable net debt (typically Debt/Equity < 1.0), forward EPS growth rates exceeding 10%, and attractive PEG ratios (generally targeted below 1.5). Finally, qualitative analysis prioritized companies with durable economic moats—such as proprietary technology, regulatory tailwinds, scale distribution networks, high switching costs, or skilled labor monopolies—benefiting from multi-year secular trends like AI data center cooling, infrastructure modernization, industrial automation, and water resource management.

== 選股方法論 ==
本觀察名單經過多階段的基本面篩選流程建立，旨在找出高品質的美國中小型股，同時排除短期動能熱門股及巨型科技股。首先，篩選條件排除標普 500 指數前 50 大成分股、市值超過 500 億美元的公司，以及主要由散戶動能或社群媒體情緒驅動的股票。其次，對公司的基本面實力進行過濾：具備正向自由現金流、可控的淨負債（通常負債權益比 < 1.0）、預估每股盈餘成長率高於 10%，以及具吸引力的本益成長比（目標通常在 1.5 以下）。最後，質化分析優先選擇具有持久經濟護城河的公司——如專利技術、法規順風、規模分銷網絡、高轉換成本或專業技術勞工壟斷——這些公司受益於 AI 資料中心冷卻、基礎設施現代化、工業自動化及水資源管理等多年結構性趨勢。

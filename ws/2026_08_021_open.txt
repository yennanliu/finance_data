== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening process designed for this watchlist systematically filtered out the S&P 500 top-50 mega-caps, high-beta momentum names, and popular retail media darlings to focus exclusively on high-quality, under-the-radar compounders. The primary universe was restricted to US-listed equities with market caps between $500 million and $50 billion. We weighted structural growth drivers—such as advanced semiconductor packaging, water infrastructure modernization, demographic aging, and industrial reshoring—against valuation, favoring companies with strong balance sheets (low debt-to-equity and positive free cash flow) and high switching costs or regulatory moats. This balanced approach ensures the list captures resilient, cash-generative businesses capable of compounding capital over a multi-year horizon regardless of short-term macroeconomic volatility.

== 選股方法論 ==
本觀察名單的篩選流程系統性地過濾了標普 500 指數前 50 大巨型股、高貝塔值的動能股以及熱門的散戶媒體寵兒，以專注於高質量、低調的複利型企業。篩選範圍嚴格限制在市值介於 5 億美元至 500 億美元之間的美國上市公司。我們將結構性成長驅動力（如先進半導體封裝、水資源基礎設施現代化、人口老齡化和工業回流）與估值進行權衡，優先選擇資產負債表強勁（低負債權益比和正自由現金流）且擁有高轉換成本或監管護城河的公司。這種平衡的方法確保了名單中的企業具備強勁的抗跌與現金生成能力，無論短期宏觀經濟如何波動，都能在多年週期中實現資本的持續複利成長。

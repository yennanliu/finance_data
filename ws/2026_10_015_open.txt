== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
This fundamental watchlist was built using a quantitative screen combined with qualitative bottom-up equity research. First, all S&P 500 top-50 mega-caps, high-beta momentum stocks, media-hyped viral tickers, and unprofitable speculative names were filtered out. Second, the universe was constrained to small- and mid-cap US equities ($500M–$50B market cap) exhibiting forward revenue growth, expanding operational margins, manageable debt levels (typically Debt/Equity < 0.8), and compelling valuation relative to earnings growth (targeting PEG < 1.5). Finally, qualitative selection favored companies with structural tailwinds (such as manufacturing reshoring, power grid modernization, building efficiency, advanced packaging, and specialty healthcare technology) and durable competitive moats (high switching costs, regulatory barriers, or proprietary technology) that remain overlooked by mainstream financial media.

== 選股方法論 ==
本基本面觀察清單係結合定量篩選與自下而上的個股研究建構而成。首先，排除 S&P 500 前 50 大巨型股、高 Beta 動能股、媒體過度炒作的熱門股以及未盈利的投機性公司。其次，將篩選範圍限制在具備預估營收成長、營運利潤率擴張、負債水準可控（通常負債權益比小於 0.8）且相較於盈餘成長估值具吸引力（目標 PEG 小於 1.5）的美國中小型股（市值 5 億美元至 500 億美元）。最後，定性選擇偏好具備結構性順風（如製造業回流、電網現代化、建築節能、先進封裝及專科醫療技術）與持久經濟護城河（高轉換成本、法規壁壘或專利技術），且仍被主流財經媒體忽視的優質企業。

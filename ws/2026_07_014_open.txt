== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
This watchlist was constructed using a multi-stage quantitative and qualitative screen designed to filter out market noise, momentum-driven hype, and overvalued mega-caps. The initial screen limited the universe to US-listed equities with market caps between $500 million and $50 billion, explicitly excluding S&P 500 top-50 components and stocks trending heavily on social media or mainstream financial news. We prioritized fundamental quality by requiring positive free cash flow, manageable debt-to-equity ratios, and a forward PEG ratio under 1.5 to balance growth and valuation. The final selection was weighted toward companies operating in high-barrier niche markets with clear structural tailwinds—such as domestic manufacturing reshoring, municipal water infrastructure upgrades, specialized medical technologies, and localized regulatory moats—ensuring resilient long-term compounding potential.

== 選股方法論 ==
本觀察名單是使用多階段定量和定性篩選構建的，旨在過濾掉市場噪音、動量驅動的炒作以及估值過高的巨型股。初始篩選將範圍限制在市值介於 5 億美元至 500 億美元之間的美國上市公司，明確排除了標普 500 指數前 50 大成分股以及在社群媒體或主流財經新聞中熱度極高的股票。我們通過要求正的自由現金流、可控的負債權益比以及低於 1.5 的預估本益成長比（PEG）來平衡成長與估值，從而優先考慮基本面質量。最終的選擇偏向於在具有明確結構性順風的高壁壘利基市場中營運的公司——例如國內製造業回流、市政水務基礎設施升級、專業醫療技術以及局部監管護城河——以確保具備韌性的長期複利潛力。

== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening process was designed to identify high-quality, resilient compounders while strictly avoiding mega-cap tech and high-sentiment momentum stocks. First, the universe was filtered to exclude companies in the top 50 of the S&P 500 and those with market caps exceeding $50B, focusing instead on the $500M to $50B sweet spot where mispricing is more common. Next, quantitative screens targeted companies with a forward PEG ratio below 1.5, positive free cash flow yields, and conservative debt-to-equity ratios. Qualitatively, we prioritized companies with clear competitive moats—such as high switching costs, distribution networks, or specialized technical expertise—and those aligned with long-term structural tailwinds (e.g., US infrastructure spending, aging demographics, and automation). This balanced approach ensures the watchlist is anchored in fundamental value while retaining strong secular growth drivers.

== 選股方法論 ==
本篩選流程旨在識別高質量、具備韌性的複合成長型企業，同時嚴格避開超大市值科技股和高熱度的動能股。首先，我們過濾掉標普 500 指數前 50 大公司以及市值超過 500 億美元的企業，將目光鎖定在 5 億至 500 億美元的中小市值黃金區間，因為此區間更容易出現定價錯誤。接著，利用定量指標篩選出預估本益成長比（PEG）低於 1.5、自由現金流殖利率為正且負債權益比例保守的公司。在定性方面，我們優先考慮具備明確競爭護城河（如高轉換成本、分銷網絡或專業技術優勢）的公司，並確保其契合長期結構性趨勢（如美國基礎設施支出、人口老齡化和自動化）。這種平衡的方法確保了觀察名單既錨定於基本面價值，又保留了強勁的長期成長驅動力。

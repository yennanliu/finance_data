== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening framework filters out hyper-leveraged, unprofitable growth, and media-hyped momentum names by enforcing strict fundamental thresholds: positive free cash flow, sustainable debt levels (D/E generally < 0.70), and valuation grounded in earnings growth (PEG targeting < 1.5). We emphasized resilient, compound growth businesses benefiting from durable structural tailwinds—such as power grid modernization, industrial reshoring/automation, data center thermal management, and healthcare outsourcing—where mispricing frequently occurs due to lack of retail media coverage.

== 選股方法論 ==
選股框架透過嚴格的基本面門檻，過濾掉高負債、未獲利的成長股以及媒體炒作的動能股：要求具備正向自由現金流、可持續的負債水準（負債權益比通常小於 0.70），以及立足於盈餘成長的估值（本益成長比目標小於 1.5）。我們著重於具備長期結構性順風（如電網升級、工業回流與自動化、資料中心熱管理以及醫療外包）的韌性複利成長企業，這類公司往往因缺乏散戶與主流媒體關注而出現顯著的估值錯置。

== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
This watchlist was constructed using a strict fundamental screening process designed to filter out market noise, momentum-driven "meme" stocks, and overvalued mega-caps. The universe was limited to US-listed companies with market capitalizations between $500 million and $50 billion. To ensure a margin of safety and strong growth potential, we prioritized companies with a forward Price-to-Earnings-to-Growth (PEG) ratio of under 1.5, positive free cash flow yields, and manageable debt-to-equity ratios. We heavily weighted structural, long-term macroeconomic tailwinds—such as the reshoring of North American manufacturing, infrastructure modernization, smart water management, and the aging US demographic—while selecting companies that possess clear, localized competitive moats (e.g., high switching costs, proprietary technology, or regulatory barriers) that protect their margins. This methodology highlights durable compounders that are currently overlooked by mainstream financial media.

== 選股方法論 ==
本觀察名單是透過嚴格的基本面篩選程序建立，旨在過濾市場噪音、動能驅動的「迷因」股以及估值過高的巨型股。篩選範圍限制在市值介於 5 億美元至 500 億美元之間的美國上市公司。為確保安全邊際和強勁的成長潛力，我們優先考慮預估本益成長比（PEG）低於 1.5、自由現金流殖利率為正且負債權益比可控的公司。我們高度重視結構性、長期的總體經濟順風——例如北美製造業回流、基礎設施現代化、智慧水資源管理以及美國人口老化——同時挑選擁有明確、在地化競爭護城河（如高轉換成本、專有技術或監管壁壘）以保護其利潤率的公司。此方法論突顯了目前被主流財經媒體忽視的耐用複利型企業。

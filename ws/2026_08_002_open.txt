== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening process designed for this watchlist prioritizes high-quality, mid-to-small-cap compounders while systematically filtering out mega-caps and speculative, media-hyped stocks. The initial filter excluded any company with a market cap exceeding $50 billion or those ranking in the top 50 of the S&P 500. To eliminate momentum-driven "meme" stocks and overvalued tech darlings, we filtered out companies with trailing 12-month beta exceeding 1.8 and those trading at forward P/E multiples above 35x, unless justified by exceptional, highly visible structural growth (resulting in a strict PEG target of under 1.5 for the vast majority of selections). Weighting was balanced between defensive value (strong free cash flow yields and low debt-to-equity) and secular growth drivers. We heavily favored companies aligned with long-term structural themes: North American industrial reshoring, grid modernization, automation, aging demographics, and highly recurring niche service models.

== 選股方法論 ==
本觀察名單的篩選過程優先考慮高質量、中小型市值的複利型企業，同時系統性地過濾掉巨型股和投機性、媒體炒作的股票。初始篩選排除了任何市值超過 500 億美元或在標普 500 指數中排名前 50 的公司。為了消除動能驅動的「迷因」股和估值過高的科技寵兒，我們過濾掉了過去 12 個月貝塔值超過 1.8 且預估本益比高於 35 倍的公司，除非有異常且高度可見的結構性增長支持（這使得絕大多數選擇的本益成長比 (PEG) 目標嚴格低於 1.5）。權重在防禦性價值（強勁的自由現金流殖利率和低負債權益比）與結構性增長驅動因素之間取得了平衡。我們強烈偏好符合長期結構性主題的公司：北美工業回流、電網現代化、自動化、人口老齡化以及高度經常性的利基服務模式。

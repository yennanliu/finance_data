== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening process began by identifying macro and sector themes expected to drive sustainable, long-term growth into 2026 and beyond, avoiding areas prone to speculative bubbles. These themes included digital transformation (enterprise software, AI enablement), industrial automation, healthcare innovation (medtech, diagnostics), critical infrastructure modernization (water, power, logistics), and specialty materials. Companies were then filtered to exclude mega-caps (S&P 500 top-50) and those frequently highlighted in mainstream financial media or exhibiting meme/momentum stock characteristics. The focus was on established businesses with strong competitive moats, such as high switching costs, network effects, proprietary technology, or regulatory barriers to entry. Quantitative screening prioritized robust fundamental growth (double-digit revenue and EPS growth), healthy balance sheets (low debt, strong free cash flow or clear path to it), and attractive valuations (PEG ratio ideally below 1.5, with some flexibility for higher-quality, higher-growth names). The final ranking emphasized a combination of conviction in the investment thesis, strength of the competitive moat, consistency of growth, and valuation, prioritizing companies with a clear path to compounding shareholder value over the long term while remaining relatively under-the-radar.

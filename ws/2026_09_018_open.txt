== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening process was designed to filter out high-sentiment, momentum-driven mega-caps and mainstream media favorites, focusing instead on high-quality compounders in the small-to-mid-cap space ($500M–$50B). We eliminated any stock in the top 50 of the S&P 500 and filtered out companies with high retail sentiment or speculative valuations. Growth and value were balanced by prioritizing companies with strong organic revenue growth, expanding operating margins, and reasonable PEG ratios (ideally <1.5, or justified by an exceptional competitive moat). Balance sheet strength was weighted heavily, favoring companies with low debt-to-equity ratios and robust free cash flow yields. The selection was heavily informed by structural macro themes, including water infrastructure modernization, factory automation, specialized healthcare services, aerospace aftermarket demand, and niche SaaS transitions.

== 選股方法論 ==
篩選過程旨在過濾掉高情緒、動能驅動的巨型股和主流媒體寵兒，轉而專注於中小型股（5 億美元至 500 億美元）中的高質量複利企業。我們排除了標準普爾 500 指數前 50 名中的任何股票，並過濾掉散戶情緒高漲或估值投機的公司。我們通過優先考慮具有強勁有機營收成長、營運利潤率擴張和合理本益成長比（理想情況下 <1.5，或由卓越的競爭護城河支撐）的公司，來平衡成長與價值。資產負債表的實力被賦予了很大的權重，偏好低負債權益比和強勁自由現金流殖利率的公司。選股深受結構性宏觀主題的啟發，包括水資源基礎設施現代化、工廠自動化、專業醫療服務、航空售後市場需求以及利基 SaaS 轉型。

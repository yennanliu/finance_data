== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
Our screening methodology focused on identifying high-quality, resilient businesses with market caps between $500M and $50B, intentionally filtering out mega-cap tech and highly speculative momentum stocks. We prioritized companies with strong structural tailwinds—such as manufacturing reshoring, infrastructure modernization, and demographic shifts—combined with high returns on capital, low leverage, and consistent free cash flow generation. Valuation was heavily weighted using the PEG ratio, targeting companies trading at a reasonable price relative to their forward earnings growth (PEG < 1.5 preferred). This balanced approach ensures we capture overlooked compounders that possess deep competitive moats, pricing power, and defensive characteristics capable of navigating macroeconomic volatility.

== 選股方法論 ==
我們的篩選方法專注於識別市值在 5 億至 500 億美元之間的高品質、具韌性的企業，刻意過濾掉超大市值科技股和高度投機的動能股。我們優先考慮具有強大結構性順風（如製造業回流、基礎設施現代化和人口結構變化）的公司，並結合高資本回報率、低槓桿和持續的自由現金流產生能力。估值方面主要使用本益成長比（PEG）進行衡量，目標是尋找交易價格相對於其預估盈餘成長而言合理的公司（PEG < 1.5 優先）。這種平衡的方法確保我們能捕捉到被忽視的複利型企業，這些企業擁有深厚的競爭護城河、定價能力以及能夠應對宏觀經濟波動的防禦性特徵。

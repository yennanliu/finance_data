== SELECTION METHODOLOGY ==
The screening process was designed to identify high-quality, resilient compounders while systematically filtering out mega-cap tech giants, speculative meme stocks, and overhyped market darlings. We restricted the universe to US-listed equities with market caps between $500 million and $50 billion. To filter out speculative momentum, we excluded any stock trading at extreme valuations (P/E > 40x) unless supported by extraordinary, highly visible growth. Instead, we prioritized companies with a PEG ratio below 1.5, positive free cash flow, and low leverage (Debt/Equity < 1.0 in most cases). The selection heavily weights structural, non-discretionary tailwinds—such as infrastructure upgrades, commercial retrofits, specialized manufacturing, and critical B2B networks—ensuring these companies possess durable competitive moats and pricing power capable of weathering macroeconomic volatility.

== 選股方法論 ==
篩選過程旨在識別高質量、具備韌性的複利型企業，同時系統性地過濾掉巨型科技巨頭、投機性迷因股和過度炒作的市場寵兒。我們將選股範圍限制在市值介於 5 億美元至 500 億美元之間的美國上市公司。為了過濾投機性動能，我們排除了任何以極端估值（本益比 > 40倍）交易的股票，除非有極其明確且非凡的增長支撐。相反，我們優先考慮本益成長比（PEG）低於 1.5、自由現金流為正且槓桿率較低（在大多數情況下負債權益比 < 1.0）的公司。選股權重高度偏向結構性、非自由裁量性的順風因素——例如基礎設施升級、商業改造、專業製造和關鍵的 B2B 網絡——以確保這些公司擁有持久的競爭護城河和定價權，能夠抵禦宏觀經濟的波動。
